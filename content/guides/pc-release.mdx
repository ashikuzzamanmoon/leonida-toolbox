GTA 6 launched on November 19, 2026 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S only. There is no PC version at launch, and Rockstar has not announced one. This guide separates what is confirmed from what is only reported.

## Confirmed: no PC version announced

As of launch day, Rockstar has announced no PC version, no PC release date and no PC system requirements. The official GTA VI page and both console store listings describe only the PS5 and Xbox Series X|S versions. Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick has spoken about Rockstar's history of console-first releases, but has not dated a PC version.

That is consistent with Rockstar's usual pattern. Every major Rockstar game in recent years has launched on console first and come to PC later.

## Reported: a PC version in early 2027?

The most widely cited report comes from GamesBeat, which reported on October 13, 2026 that four industry sources said Rockstar was targeting a PC release in Q1 2027, meaning January to March. Rockstar has not confirmed this, and it should be treated as a report, not a date.

Hardware analyst Geekerwan (via Notebookcheck, November 23) estimates that a Ryzen 7 9700X with an RTX 5070-class GPU could run the game at 1440p and 60fps, while older mainstream PCs would likely sit near 30fps and lean on frame generation. This is a hardware analysis, not a Rockstar statement, and there is still no official PC date or requirements.

Other estimates differ widely:

- Analyst Michael Pachter has suggested May to June 2027.
- GameSpot has floated Q1 2028 as a possibility.
- Some creators, working from Rockstar's past gaps, estimate late 2027 or early 2028.

None of these is confirmed. Until Rockstar or Take-Two says something on the record, there is no reliable PC date.

## How long PC players waited before

Rockstar's previous console-to-PC gaps give a sense of the range:

| Game | Console to PC gap |
| --- | --- |
| GTA IV | About 7 months |
| GTA V | About 19 months |
| Red Dead Redemption 2 | About 13 months |

A Q1 2027 PC launch would be a much shorter wait than any of these, roughly four months after the console launch. That is possible, but it would be a break from Rockstar's history, which is one reason to treat the report cautiously.

## What to watch out for

With no official PC version, fake downloads are already circulating. Rockstar has announced no PC beta, no early access and no launcher. Any site offering a GTA 6 PC download, installer or "port" before an official announcement is a scam, and several reports have warned of malware in unofficial downloads. Unofficial workarounds also cannot offer real PC features like DLSS, FSR or ray tracing settings.

## Should you wait or buy a console version?

That depends on how much you want to play now. The console versions are the only way to play today, and they are well reviewed: the game holds a 99 on Metacritic. See [PS5 vs Xbox Series X|S](/guides/platforms-ps5-xbox) to compare the console versions.

If you do buy on console and later move to PC, note that Rockstar has said nothing about cross-progression between console and PC. See [Saves and Cross-Progression](/guides/saves-cross-progression) for the current state of saves.
