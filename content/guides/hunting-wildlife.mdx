Leonida is alive. Rockstar's pre-launch figure was over 170 animal species, and the finished game's animal log tracks **255 entries**, including every dog and cat breed and every hunting target. Wildlife is not just decoration: studying animals counts toward 100% completion, and hunting is a full questline with its own character, rewards and trophy.

## The animal log

The log lives under Progress, with tabs for Hunting, Fishing, Mammals, Cats & Dogs, Reptiles & Amphibians, Birds, Aquatic and Other. The counts break down as:

| Group | Entries |
| --- | --- |
| Birds | 60 |
| Fish | 35 |
| Cats and dogs | 34 |
| Mammals | 18 |
| Aquatic animals | 18 |
| Reptiles | 6 |
| Other | 23 |
| Hunting entries | 61 |

To log an animal, equip the **binoculars** from the weapon wheel, zoom in, and hold the study button while the animal is in view. No killing is required. **100% completion needs any 100 of the 255 studied**, and fish and birds count too, so the easiest path is to study everything you see from the start.

New to Rockstar games are flamingos, manatees, pelicans and alligators, plus invasive green iguanas, cane toads, feral cats and rhesus macaques. Rockstar said before launch that animals come in **Rare, Discoverable and Legendary** tiers, but which animals fall into each tier is still not documented. We will list them once verified in the game.

## Hunting: Bret Vogel's Target Decks

Hunting is a **Jason-only** activity, built around Bret Vogel, Jason's old army friend, who runs a hunting business called Killtelligence near Mount Kalaga.

- **How it starts:** some time after the Chapter 2 mission Coffee Break, Jason gets a text from Bret that marks the first mission, Veteran Hunted, on the map in Lummox County. You must be playing as Jason, and he goes alone. The mission pays $500 and gives you Bret's hunting sniper rifle, the Killtelligence SM-69.
- **Target Decks:** after Veteran Hunted, Bret hands out **15 Target Decks**, each a set of animals to hunt. Hunting spots appear on the map with purple icons. All 15 decks are required for 100% completion.
- **The questline stays open after the story ends**, so there is no rush.

## How a hunt works

Every hunt follows the same steps:

1. Use **Focus** to show the animal's scent trail as a white line, and follow it. The line turns yellow as you get close.
2. Study the animal with binoculars or a scope to confirm it is your target.
3. Shoot it with a hunting rifle. **One shot to the head or heart** leaves it in pristine condition. Every extra shot lowers the quality, and too many ruin it.
4. Pick up the carcass and stow it in the truck.
5. Drive it back and drop it off at Bret's processing area.

Bret only accepts carcasses in **Pristine or Good** condition, so weapon choice matters. A scoped rifle suits larger animals, and a small-calibre rifle suits turkeys and skunks. Clean kills pay more.

Early decks ask for whitetail deer around Mount Kalaga, wild boar and turkey near Adler Creek and Bonanza, and cane toads, nutria and Nile monitors in the Buckskin swamps and Pelican Valley. Skunks only come out after dark, and boar rest in shady woodland by day.

## Where to find wildlife

- **Mount Kalaga National Park.** The center of hunting: forests, rivers and the Abandoned Concrete Plant area. Rockstar describes it as offering prime hunting, fishing and off-road trails.
- **Grassrivers.** Alligators, wading birds and airboat hunting in the swamps.
- **Leonida Keys.** Iguanas, pelicans, dolphins, sea turtles and rays. See [Leonida Keys & Islands](/guides/leonida-keys).

Hunting a unique animal for Bret unlocks the **Fair Game** trophy. See [Trophies & Achievements](/guides/trophies-achievements).

## Fishing

Fishing is **catch and release**, with **35 fish species** across fresh and salt water. Fish entries in the animal log fill in when you catch the fish. There is also a fishing challenge against a character named Cal, tied to the "Baited My Tackle" trophy.
