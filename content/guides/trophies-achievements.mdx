GTA 6 has **55 trophies on PlayStation and 55 achievements on Xbox**: 1 Platinum, 2 Gold, 6 Silver and 46 Bronze. On Xbox, the Platinum equivalent is a Mythic Achievement. The good news for completionists: every trophy can be earned in a single playthrough, none require online play, and nothing is missable for 100% completion. The bad news: expect a long road. Push Square estimates the Platinum takes 300 hours or more.

Spoiler warning: 28 of the 55 trophies are hidden because their descriptions reveal story details. This guide names the story trophies by chapter only, and lists the non-hidden ones in full.

## The big ones

| Trophy | Grade | What it takes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vice Got a Price! | Platinum | Earn every other trophy. |
| A Long Road | Gold | Reach 100% completion using the in-game checklist. |
| You Gotta Know When to Get Off | Gold | Complete the final story mission. |
| Cunning Stunts | Silver | Complete 250 stunts across Leonida. |
| The Shootist | Silver | Earn a gold medal in all shooting range challenges (42 gold medals). |
| Strangers & Freaks | Silver | Complete 8 stranger mission strands across Leonida. |
| Don't Touch My Deck | Silver | Complete 3 collectible card sets. |
| You WAiNKer! | Silver | Scan 100 locations with the WAiNK app. |
| Maximum Withdrawal | Silver | Rob 5 bank vaults and escape. |

## Story trophies

These unlock naturally as you play, one per chapter plus mission-specific ones: Just Like Paradise (complete the opening mission, Gone Fishing), Paradise Lost (Chapter 1), Vice City, Baby! (Chapter 3), Top Of The World, Ma (Chapter 4), and the final-mission Gold above. Several more are tied to specific story missions such as The Cartel Heist, Headliners and the VCIA Takedown.

## The long grinds

The trophies that take the most time are the checklist ones. Our [100% checklist tool](/checklist) tracks them:

- **A Long Road** needs the full 100% checklist: story, collectibles, activities and discoveries. The checklist only becomes visible under Progress > Stats after the story, but progress made during the story counts.
- **Cunning Stunts** needs 250 stunts, and the 100% checklist asks for 300.
- **The Shootist** needs gold medals in all shooting range challenges.
- **One Last Thing** needs 50 of the game's 80 challenges completed.
- **Master of None** needs you to try each of the nine activity types at least once.

## Activity and collection trophies

- **Top Fun:** win a Top Fun drone race at all 14 locations.
- **Earn Cash, Not Kudos:** win 20 different races.
- **Baited My Tackle:** beat Cal in a fishing challenge.
- **Whip It Good:** discover 100 vehicles.
- **On the Spot:** register 10 vehicles to the Scooter Bros app.
- **Little Boxes:** purchase all eight purchasable garages. See [Safehouses](/guides/safehouses).
- **Treat Yo Self:** spend $100,000 across shops.
- **Dime on the Dollar:** launder $250,000 in Marked Bills through contacts.
- **Positive Mental Attitude:** meditate at all 18 meditation spots.

## Stranger mission trophies

Each stranger strand ends with its own trophy when you finish their final mission: Andy (Road to Rehabilitation), Kenton (This Was No Boat Accident), Billy (The Lone Rider), Randy (Supply & Demand) and Tyrone (Spin On This!).

## Warnings

- **Cheats disable trophies.** Using any cheat blocks trophies and achievements for that session, and blocks saving too. See [Cheats](/guides/cheats).
- **The Truth Is Out There** is a hidden Bronze trophy with an unconfirmed unlock method, tied to a UFO event players are still working out. See [Easter Eggs](/guides/easter-eggs).
- **Ten Pin** is another hidden trophy whose exact method is still being confirmed.
