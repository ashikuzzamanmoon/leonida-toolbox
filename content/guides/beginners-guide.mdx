Grand Theft Auto VI launched on November 19, 2026 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. It is a huge game set in Leonida, Rockstar's fictional Florida, and its first hours throw a lot of systems at you at once. This spoiler-free guide covers the basics you need before you start.

## The opening hours

The story begins with a prologue as Lucia, then switches to Jason in the Leonida Keys. You will spend the first hours in Key Lento, Jason's home, doing small jobs and learning the controls. The game opens up gradually: many activities, including selling stolen cars and fast travel, stay locked until you are partway through the first chapter, so focus on the story missions early on.

You play as both Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos, but free switching between them unlocks later. See [How Character Switching Works](/guides/character-switching) for the details.

## Healing and Zombix

There is no regenerating health in the traditional sense. Your main healing item is Zombix, a small pill bottle you can carry up to five of. Open your loadout and use the on-screen prompt to take one when you are hurt. You pick up your first Zombix in the opening mission, and grocery and convenience stores sell more. Keep a few on you before any mission with shooting in it.

Food, drinks and medicine also give small stat bonuses, so it is worth eating before a tough job.

## Money: spend it carefully

Cash is tight at the start, and you will need it for mission objectives, not just shopping. Two rules to learn early:

- **If you die with cash on you, you lose it.** Stash money under the loose floorboards in Jason's bedroom as soon as you earn it. Money in the stash is safe.
- **Early mission payments arrive in Jason's mailbox** outside his house, until a later mission opens a bank account. Check it after jobs.

Do not worry about getting rich in the first hours. Story missions pay enough to keep you going. For the longer game, see the [Money Guide](/guides/money-guide).

## The police and wanted levels

GTA 6 tracks what the police know about you: your clothes, your hat and glasses, and your car. Icons on screen show which of these the police have identified. Changing any of them drops that part of your description. A police scanner app arrives early and lets you listen to police radio during a chase.

To escape, get out of the flashing search area on the minimap. Driving fast and then going off-road works well, and ducking while driving (Circle on PS5, B on Xbox) makes you harder to spot.

## Controls and difficulty

GTA 6 has a new default control scheme: run is mapped to L3 and jump to Cross/X. If you prefer the GTA 5 layout, go to Settings, then Control Scheme, and switch to Classic.

There are no traditional difficulty settings, but Settings, then Gameplay, offers two assists near the bottom of the list: Accessible Combat, which reduces the damage you take and increases the damage enemies take, and Reduced Vehicle Damage, which makes your car tougher in crashes and combat.

## Getting around

There is no traditional fast travel. Instead, open your phone (Up on the D-pad) and use the RydeMe app to pay for a ride to your waypoint. It costs a small fee and becomes available partway through Chapter 1.

When you are unsure what to do next, open the map (Touch Pad on PS5) and press Right to see a list of available story missions, with mandatory objectives at the top. You can set a waypoint to any mission from there.

## A few things worth knowing

- **Focus mode** slows time in shootouts and lets you target weak spots. Outside combat it does not drain, and it highlights nearby loot, so use it while exploring.
- **Stealing cars is a minigame.** When breaking in with a Slim Jim, a Clone Key or a hotwire, look for the pink sweet spot to finish faster.
- **Missions cannot be replayed.** Once a story mission is done, there is no way to run it again on the same save, so keep a manual save before big moments if you want to revisit them.
- **Your reputation follows your violence.** Quiet takedowns and non-lethal shots build a Professional or Hardened reputation; killing builds a Psychopath one. Your criminal profile is one of the things that shapes the ending, so play the way you want to be remembered.
