GTA 6 brings back Strangers and Freaks, the series' optional side questlines, alongside a wider set of side missions and activities. This guide covers the questlines confirmed so far, which ones count toward the Strangers and Freaks trophy, and what is missable. The full list is still being mapped, so expect updates.

## How side missions start

There is no single side mission menu. Questlines start in one of three ways:

- **Flyers and notice boards**, such as Kirk's Skunk Ape posters in Key Lento.
- **Phone calls and texts** from characters you met during the story, such as Kenton, Andy, Bret and Yago.
- **Marked spots on the map**, such as Billy's mud club or Kenton's dive sites.

Unlocked side missions sit on the map alongside story missions. Side missions carry a black icon with pink letters, while smaller side tasks use a purple icon. A side mission with an **hourglass icon is timed**, so do it as soon as it appears.

Most questlines stay available after the story in free roam, and completed optional missions are not listed anywhere, so it is worth keeping track yourself.

## The Strangers and Freaks trophy

The Strangers and Freaks trophy requires completing **8 qualifying questlines**. Not every side questline counts, and the game does not tell you which ones do. Based on early trophy tracking:

**Counts toward the trophy:** Kirk (Skunk Ape hunt), Kyle (McKittrick Farm), Yago (street races) and Pilot Pete. More qualifying questlines are still being identified.

**Has its own trophy instead:** Kenton (treasure dives), Billy (mud club), Andy (Cellerica jobs), Tyrone and Bret (hunting). These do not count toward Strangers and Freaks.

Check the trophy's progress counter after each questline to confirm.

## Questlines by chapter

### Chapter 1

- **Kirk, the Skunk Ape hunt.** Start from Skunk Ape flyers around Key Lento; Kirk waits in Pelican Valley near Thorp Adventures in the evening. Three missions: Search for the Skunk Ape, Search for Tree Effigies, and Help Kirk Find Mr. Mangrove. Some missions are night only, and the final one is Lucia only. Counts toward the trophy.
- **Shirley, Jason's neighbor.** Two small errands in the Keys: Get Shirley's Rum and Steal the Scooter. **Both are missable and Chapter 1 exclusive.** Do them before finishing the chapter. They do not count toward the trophy.
- **Kenton, treasure dives.** Start at his dive shop in Key Lento after the Island Dreams story mission. Wrecks show as purple chest icons. Completing seven dive sets earns its own trophy. Does not count toward Strangers and Freaks.
- **Cal, fishing.** Beat Cal in a fishing challenge to earn his trophy. Does not count toward Strangers and Freaks.

### Chapter 2

- **Kyle McKittrick, crop duster jobs.** Start from a pilot job flyer, then McKittrick Farm. Three missions: Check Out McKittrick Farm, Collect Chemicals for Kyle, and Buddy? The last one lands in Chapter 3. Counts toward the trophy.
- **Billy, the Thrillbilly Mud Club.** Start at the mud club in Kelly County after the Coffee Break story mission. A string of races and crew jobs, including Thrillbillies and Twist Wrist, Smash, Grab. Finishing the chain earns its own trophy, The Lone Rider. Does not count toward Strangers and Freaks.
- **Bret Vogel, hunting.** Jason only. Starts with Veteran Hunted. Hunting a unique animal for Bret earns the Fair Game trophy. Does not count toward Strangers and Freaks.
- **Moses, vehicle theft.** Steal vehicles for Moses. Does not count toward the trophy.

### Chapter 3 and later

- **Yago, street races.** Timed races, marked with an hourglass icon. Do each one as soon as it shows up, or you may miss it. Some races are Jason only. Counts toward the trophy.
- **Andy, Cellerica jobs.** Lucia only. A chain of initiation tasks for the Cellerica group, including van and cleanup work. Finishing the chain earns its own trophy. Does not count toward Strangers and Freaks.
- **Pilot Pete.** Jason only. Recover Pete's plane in The Last Mile: Passing the Torch. Counts toward the trophy.
- **Tyrone and Yasmani.** Questlines involving Kitty Litter Labs and other Vice City contacts. Tyrone's chain has its own trophy. Neither counts toward Strangers and Freaks, based on current tracking.

## Tips

- **Do Shirley's errands early.** They are the only confirmed missable questlines, and they vanish after Chapter 1.
- **Watch for the hourglass.** Timed missions, especially Yago's races, expire if you put them off.
- **Mind the character locks.** Some missions need Jason or Lucia specifically. Switch with the Partner Menu; see [How Character Switching Works](/guides/character-switching).
- **Wait for callbacks.** After finishing a side quest, the next one in the chain can take a few in-game days to appear. Keep playing other content in the meantime.
