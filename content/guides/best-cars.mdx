GTA 6 launched with a large vehicle roster, including 6 supercars and around 25 sports cars. Rockstar has not published official top speeds or performance stats for any vehicle, so every ranking right now is an early estimate based on community testing and GTA Online data for returning cars. Expect this list to change as cars are properly timed in-game.

All figures are estimates from community databases, not tested in-game numbers. Different sources disagree on the exact order, particularly below the top two, so treat the lower rankings as provisional.

## The fastest cars

The **Grotti Itali RSX** is the current pick for fastest car in the game, a Ferrari SF90-style sports car with all-wheel drive. The **Pegassi Tempesta** is the fastest car in the dedicated Super class. Behind them sit the **Progen Emerus** (McLaren Senna-style), **Pegassi Zorrusso** and **Grotti Furia** (Ferrari 488 Pista-style), all estimated above 120 mph without upgrades. The **Truffade Thrax**, based on the Bugatti Divo, is the only true hypercar spotted so far.

For the full ranked list and how the estimates were made, see [Fastest Car in GTA 6](/guides/fastest-car).

## Best early cars

For most of Chapter 1 you are locked into Jason's starting vehicle, the **'70 Vapid Ganado** pickup, so early car choice is not really a thing. Once registration opens in Chapter 2, the best early pick is whatever solid sports car you can steal without a tracker: the **Karin Sultan**, **Invetero Coquette** and **Dinka Sugoi** are all strong, common and cheap to register. Avoid falling in love with a supercar early. Almost all of them carry trackers, and advanced trackers cannot be removed until later in the story.

## How to get and keep a car

- **Steal it, then register it.** Registration unlocks in Chapter 2 through Moses at Watkins Auto Parts in Port Gellhorn. Unregistered stolen cars are lost when you die, get busted or leave them.
- **Scan first.** The WAINK phone app shows a parked car's security level, tracker status, resale value and registration cost before you commit to stealing it.
- **Mind the tracker.** Cars with standard trackers can be dealt with from Chapter 3 onward; advanced trackers take longer. If a car reports itself, the police know where you are.
- **Start with three slots.** You begin with room for three personal vehicles. Buy garages through the Vice City Real Estate app to hold more. See [Businesses & Properties in GTA 6](/guides/businesses-properties).
- **Fuel matters.** Every vehicle has a fuel gauge and an engine condition gauge. Refueling at a gas station takes about 10 seconds.

## Best cars for specific jobs

- **Getaways:** a fast motorcycle beats any car through traffic, but among cars the Itali RSX and Tempesta are the obvious picks.
- **Off-road:** the Annis Hellion and the Ultimate Edition's '67 Vapid Dominator Buggy are built for the mud and trails of Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga.
- **Lugging loot:** bigger vehicles hold more stolen goods. Vans and trucks carry more bags than a sports car, and motorcycles are useless for large hauls.

Two wheels are often the better tool in Vice City traffic. See [Best Motorcycles in GTA 6](/guides/best-motorcycles), and for making a car your own, [Vehicle Customization in GTA 6](/guides/vehicle-customization).
