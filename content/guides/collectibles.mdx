GTA 6 has one of the biggest collectible hunts in the series, and most types feed into the 100% completion checklist. The good news: the checklist is forgiving. Most categories only ask for part of the total, and nothing on it is permanently missable. Everything can be finished in free roam after the story.

Track everything under **Progress > Collections** in the pause menu. The 100% checklist itself appears under Progress > Stats only after you finish the story, but progress made during the story already counts. Use our [100% checklist tool](/checklist) to tick items off as you go.

## Collectible cards

The biggest hunt in the game: **18 sets containing a reported 480 unique cards**, scattered across Leonida in buildings, on rooftops and in yellow phone boxes.

- Track them at Progress > Collections > Collectible Cards.
- Cards come in color variants (Gold, Silver, Bronze). Each variant fills its own slot, and duplicates of the same variant do not count.
- 100% completion needs any **15 of the 18 sets**. The "Don't Touch My Deck" trophy needs 3 sets.
- Cards can be sold at pawn shops for $20 to over $1,000 depending on rarity, without losing your collection progress.
- One early heads-up: the Vicki card from the Delights Dancers set sits in the Starlet Motel office in Port Gellhorn, and that interior becomes inaccessible after Chapter 2. Since 100% only needs 15 of 18 sets, missing it does not block completion.

The 18 sets include sports teams (Vice City Flamingos, Liberty City Swingers, Los Santos Panic), dancer sets (Delights Dancers, Jack of Hearts Dancers, Nine1Nine Dancers) and comic sets (The Legends of Ratface, Ed Comics Universe). Individual card locations are still being mapped by the community.

## WAiNK locations

The WAiNK app on your phone scans points of interest while you are on foot. When a scannable spot is nearby, you get an on-screen prompt. There are four kinds:

- **Landmarks** (31 reported), such as towers and notable buildings.
- **Curiosities** (35 reported), odd objects and features around Leonida.
- **Sculptures** (23 reported), statues and art installations. Washington Beach alone holds three: Neptune Rises, the Vice Museum of Art sculpture and the Princess Robot Bubblegum statue.
- **No Flusher tags** (23 reported), utility markings on the ground.

100% completion and the "You WAiNKer!" trophy both ask for **100 scans** in total. WAiNK also scans vehicles, tattoos in magazines, and space debris, which unlocks a cheat filter once all of it is scanned.

## Luxury watches

**34 luxury watches** are hidden around Leonida, usually at gang operation sites. They show a golden icon when you look at them. They count toward the 79 "Discovered Styles" on the 100% checklist (any 70 needed), alongside tattoo magazines you scan with WAiNK. Finding 10 watches unlocks the cheat that moves time forward, and 20 unlocks the cheat that pauses time of day. See [Cheats](/guides/cheats).

## Magazines, books and comics

**34 items** in total: 27 magazines and 7 book swaps. 100% completion needs 30 of the 34. Some sit in Jason's house in Key Lento, so grab them early in Chapter 1 while you are there.

## Sunken treasure and underwater discoveries

Kenton Rees, a treasure hunter in the Keys, sends you diving for treasure after the Chapter 1 mission Island Dreams. There are **7 treasure sets** around Key Lento's six shipwrecks (100% needs 5), plus **33 underwater discoveries** to find (100% needs all 33). See [Leonida Keys & Islands](/guides/leonida-keys).

## Other collectibles

- **Unique playlists:** 2 posters you scan with WAiNK to unlock new music playlists. 100% needs both.
- **Space debris:** scanned with WAiNK. Scanning all of it unlocks the Powdered Sugar screen filter cheat.

## Tips for the hunt

- Scan vehicles and locations as you go. The 100% checklist also asks for 200 vehicles scanned, and doing it during the story saves a long cleanup.
- Clear gang operations when you pass them. They often hold luxury watches and other valuables.
- Sell duplicate cards at pawn shops. It is free money and your collection progress is unaffected.
