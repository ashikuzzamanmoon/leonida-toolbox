Vice City is the beating heart of GTA 6: a neon metropolis on Leonida's southeast coast, in Vice-Dale County, and Rockstar's modern take on Miami. The studio calls it "the sun and fun capital of America," and it is where most of the story's second half plays out. This guide covers the districts, the key landmarks, and how to make the most of the city.

For the wider state, see the [GTA 6 Map guide](/guides/map-guide). For where Jason and Lucia live once they reach the city, see [Safehouses](/guides/safehouses).

## The districts

Rockstar's official description names four places inside Vice City, and the official printable map labels several more neighborhoods:

- **Ocean Beach.** The signature beachfront strip: pastel art deco hotels, white sand, tourists, volleyball players and street performers. It is modeled on Miami Beach's Ocean Drive, and the Ocean View Hotel from the original GTA: Vice City returns here.
- **Little Cuba.** The cultural heart of inland Vice City, inspired by Miami's Little Havana. Rockstar highlights its bustling panaderias (Cuban bakeries), family businesses and street vendors.
- **Tisha-Wocka.** The flea market district, home to what Rockstar calls "bootleg brands."
- **Port Vice City (VC Port).** The cruise and cargo port east of downtown, billed by Rockstar as a cruise ship capital.
- **Downtown.** The high-rise financial core, home to the Sahara Arena and Sinfrontera National Bank.
- **Washington Beach and Vice Beach.** The residential beachfront neighborhoods on the barrier island east of the mainland city, quieter than Ocean Beach.
- **La Perle.** A lower-income neighborhood with Haitian and Caribbean influences, inspired by Miami's Little Haiti.
- **Stockyard.** The arts district of street murals and converted warehouses, modeled on Wynwood.
- **Rockridge, Salton, Tequesta and Crosstown.** Urban neighborhoods across the city, each with its own mix of housing, shops and hangouts. Tequesta has the city's tallest towers.

Vice City International Airport (VCIA) sits southwest of downtown, and it plays a role in the story.

## What to do in Vice City

The city is the densest part of Leonida for activities and side content:

- **Nightlife.** The Jack of Hearts strip club and the NINE1NINE nightclub are among the city's big venues, tied to the character Boobie Ike.
- **Shopping and services.** Clothing stores, Ammu-Nation gun stores, gyms, barbers and tattoo parlors are spread across the districts. Shops are also where the "Treat Yo Self" trophy progress builds up.
- **Races and stunts.** Street races, Top Fun drone races and stunt jumps are scattered through the city. Winning races counts toward several trophies.
- **Collectibles.** Vice City hides a large share of the game's [collectible cards](/guides/collectibles), WAiNK landmarks and sculptures. Washington Beach alone holds several sculptures you can scan.
- **Secrets.** Players have already found a Dexter-inspired apartment, the Trinity Apartments, on Washington Island. See [Easter Eggs](/guides/easter-eggs) for details.

## Getting around the city

Vice City traffic is dense and the police presence is heavier than in the countryside. A few practical notes:

- Use the **RydeMe app** on your phone to cross the city quickly instead of driving through downtown traffic.
- **Garages** are the property worth buying here. Several of the eight purchasable garages are in or near the city, including the most expensive one, Atlantic Court Garage in Ocean Beach. See [Safehouses](/guides/safehouses).
- Crimes need a witness or an alarm to bring the police, so a quiet job in a side street is safer than the same job on a crowded beachfront. See [Tips & Tricks](/guides/tips-tricks).
