GTA 6 launched on November 19, 2026, and the first days brought a handful of real problems alongside the usual launch noise. The biggest one is a PS5 crash with error code CE-108255-1. Rockstar has not named a cause or a fix yet, and no post-launch patch has been released. This guide collects what is confirmed, the fixes worth trying, and the steps that waste your time. Last checked November 23, 2026.

## Current patch status

- PS5: 01.002.000 (November 13, about 1 GB), then 01.002.001 (November 16, about 602 MB). Xbox got the same updates (about 2.2 GB and 1.6 GB).
- Rockstar has published no changelog for either update, so what they changed is not public.
- As of November 23, no post-launch patch has been announced. Version 01.002.001 is still the version everyone is playing.
- A November 25 patch date is circulating on social media, but no official Rockstar or PlayStation channel has confirmed it. Treat it as a rumour, not a date.
- Separate from the game patch: PS5 system software 26.07-14.23.00 (released November 17, about 1.27 GB) is required before GTA 6 will start. If the game will not boot at all, check this first.

## PS5 crash: error CE-108255-1

- Since launch, PS5 players report the game closing to the home screen with error CE-108255-1. Some hit it right after the opening cutscene, others after 30-40 minutes of play. Crashes have also been reported at the same story points repeatedly: Lucia's prison sequence (even after skipping the opening cutscene) and Jason's section near the boat. Reports cover base, Digital, and Slim PS5 models, plus at least one PS5 Pro.
- The code itself is not GTA 6 specific. Sony describes it as a general error with a game or application, with no specific cause given.
- Rockstar has not named a cause or a fix. Its support guidance sends console errors to PlayStation and Xbox support.
- Players who cleared the cache, rebuilt the database, reinstalled the game, or restored licences report the crash coming back. A full console reset has not fixed it for anyone quoted in reports either.

## Other PS5 error codes

GTA 6 has no error codes of its own — these are Sony's codes, and Rockstar has acknowledged none of them. The meanings and fixes below are Sony's own words from the PlayStation Support error-code pages.

| Code | What Sony says it means | Sony's fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CE-107891-6 | Content isn't installing properly | Delete the game (Settings > Storage) and reinstall it |
| CE-109258-5 | Unable to connect to the server — the game's licence could not be confirmed | Check the PlayStation status page, restart the PS5, then restore licences (Settings > Users and Accounts > Other > Restore Licences) |
| CE-109245-1 | The licence information of the content can't be confirmed | Restart the PS5, sign in with the account that bought the game, and turn on Console Sharing and Offline Play |
| NP-102944-0 | Please install the latest system software | Update the PS5 system software — GTA 6 needs version 26.07-14.23.00 or later |

## Fixes worth trying, in order

1. Install the GTA 6 update: highlight GTA 6 on the home screen, press Options, choose Check for Update. The launch update is 01.002.001, about 602 MB. Pre-load copies may not have installed it yet.
2. Update the PS5 system software: Settings > System > System Software > System Software Update and Settings. GTA 6 needs version 26.07-14.23.00 or later.
3. Close the game fully and restart the console: close GTA 6 from the control centre, then Power > Turn Off PS5 (not Rest Mode). Wait for the light to go out, then start it again.
4. Clear the cache and rebuild the database (Safe Mode): turn the PS5 off completely, hold the power button until the second beep (about 7 seconds), connect the controller with a USB cable, press the PS button, then choose Clear Cache and Rebuild Database. This does not delete games or saves.
5. Give the console air: make sure the vents have space on all sides.
6. If GTA 6 is installed on an M.2 expansion SSD, move it to the internal drive and test there (Settings > Storage).
7. Reinstall the game as a last resort. Deleting the game leaves saved data on the console. Downloading the whole game again takes a long time, so try the steps above first.

## What not to do

- Do not factory reset your PS5 for this error. Players who did it say it did not help, and a reset erases everything on the console.
- Do not delete your saved data. Saves are not the problem, and deleting them loses your progress for nothing.
- Do not pay for a repair yet unless other games crash with the same code. If only GTA 6 crashes, the fix most likely comes from Rockstar in a patch.

## Lost progress and saving

- Some players report losing hours of progress after a crash, or after switching the console off mid-session, even with autosave on.
- The game saves after main story missions and larger side missions, and never during a mission. Autosave does not appear in Save Game; after a crash, look for it in Load Game.
- Save by hand before and after missions, and always before quitting. That way a crash costs minutes, not hours.
- See the [Saves & Cross-Progression](/guides/saves-cross-progression) guide for how saving works.

## Other reported issues

- PlayStation Network went down on November 18, hours before the US unlock, and was restored the same afternoon. Sony gave no cause. Services are back to normal.
- Xbox Quick Resume is intermittent: at least one hands-on session reported the game failing to resume from sleep and returning the player to before the last checkpoint, losing about ten minutes.
- Frame rate: Digital Foundry measured sustained drops to 23-24 frames per second in heavy action on the base PS5, with the Pro holding 30 and the Series S in the mid-20s. There is no 60fps mode; Rockstar has said the game is CPU-bound, with no plans for one even on PS5 Pro. See [Performance Settings](/guides/performance-settings).
- Physical copies: the boxed edition contains a download code, not a disc. Some buyers received no code, or one that did not work. Rockstar's advice is to contact the retailer.
- Small bugs: reviewers report minor visual issues, such as characters clipping through objects and pedestrians getting stuck. Rockstar has acknowledged one bug that makes the vignette effect stronger than intended; no fix date is known.

If the game keeps crashing for you, note the exact spot where it happens and report it to Rockstar Support. Detailed reports are how a game bug gets fixed. This guide will be updated when Rockstar announces a patch or a fix.
