The fastest way to earn money in GTA 6 changes as the story progresses. There is no single best method for the whole game, so this guide covers the quickest earner at each stage, plus the traps to avoid.

## Chapter 1: take what comes, don't grind

Money farming in Chapter 1 is wasted effort because you lose your cash at the end of the chapter. Play the story and pick up easy money along the way:

- **Store stick-ups.** Aim at the clerk, empty the register. Every grocery store in the game can be robbed, and it takes seconds.
- **Gang operations.** Yellow icons on the map mark gang hideouts holding drugs, cash and safes. Clearing one pays out once you sell the loot to a fence.
- **Races.** Winning races pays and costs nothing to enter.

## Chapter 2: the Moses car loop

Selling stolen cars to Moses is the best early money loop. It unlocks early in Chapter 2 after the mission where you take a vehicle to him.

1. Walk to the main road in Port Gellhorn and steal a car without a tracker. Moses refuses cars stolen right next to his garage, and he will not take tracked vehicles at all.
2. Drive it to Moses at Watkins Auto Parts and sell it. Expect $1,000 to $5,000 per car.
3. Wait out the cooldown of around two minutes, then repeat.

Each sale takes one or two minutes, which makes this the fastest reliable income available at this stage. It is easily enough to cover clothes and weapons, but don't try to farm a fortune yet. Much better methods open in Chapter 3.

## Chapter 3: jewelry and banks

Chapter 3 introduces the bigger jobs, and this is where farming becomes worthwhile.

- **Jewelry stores and pawn shops** pay $1,000 to $25,000 each. Many can be entered from the back, where you can disable the security system or destroy CCTV cameras before grabbing the goods.
- **Payday loan stores** (7 total) pay $2,000 to $25,000. The Little Cuba location pays the most.
- **Small banks** (14 total) pay $5,000 to $45,000.
- **Gang hideouts** remain excellent. The drug type matters: crystal and cocaine sell for far more than weed, so prioritize hideouts holding the expensive stuff and sell to Tyrone.

Wait until you have the dye pack jammer from the Chapter 3 story before robbing banks. Without it, the dye packs in bank cash explode and ruin the take.

## End of Chapter 4: bank vaults

The fastest money in the game by a wide margin is robbing large bank vaults, unlocked near the end of Chapter 4 through the optional quest "That's Where The Money Is..." (wait at the cafe). Each vault pays $65,000 to $205,000 for about five minutes of work. Follow a security van to a bank, enter the vault while the guard carries money in, then escape on a fast motorcycle. You get 4 stars instantly, so switch vehicles once you break line of sight.

Yasmani sells security van tips for $10,000 after you complete his two payday loan jobs, which removes the waiting around. In the endgame, repeated vault robberies earn roughly $100,000 to $200,000 every few minutes.

## Money mistakes to avoid

- **Don't hoard cash.** Deposit earnings at an ATM. Cash on your person is lost when you are Wasted or Busted.
- **Don't farm Chapter 1.** You lose the money at the end of the chapter.
- **Don't rob banks early.** Dye packs destroy the take until you have the jammer.
- **Don't sell damaged cars to Moses.** Damage lowers the price, so drive carefully on the way there.
