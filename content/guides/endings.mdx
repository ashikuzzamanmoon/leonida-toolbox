**Spoiler warning:** this guide avoids describing what happens in any ending, but it does discuss the late-game systems that shape them. If you consider even that a spoiler, come back after you finish the story.

## Does GTA 6 have multiple endings?

Yes. Rockstar has confirmed to Game8 that the campaign has "a lot more than two" possible endings, and that the team does not consider any of them bad: they are different conclusions based on the choices you made during the story.

How many endings there are in total is still unknown. Reviewers who have finished the game report at least four. GameSpot's players witnessed two firsthand and believe there are more. Kotaku's reviewer saw two endings across a 152-hour playthrough. The exact number is still being mapped, and this page will be updated as players find more.

## How GTA 6's endings differ from GTA 5

GTA 5 settled its ending with a single choice in the final mission, offering three options. GTA 6 works differently: there is no final menu choice. Instead, the ending is shaped by how you played the whole game. Your decisions across the story, your relationship, your conduct and your money all feed into the outcome.

## The four factors that shape your ending

Rockstar has confirmed four factors that affect how the story wraps up:

### 1. Narrative choices

Key decisions made across the story, such as whether you spare or eliminate certain characters during missions. These are the big story beats, and they carry forward to the finale.

### 2. Lucia and Jason's relationship

Whether the pair grow into a committed couple or stay strictly partners in crime. Rockstar describes this as believable progression based on your overall investment in the relationship. You can build it up through dates and time spent together, or let it go cold. A good ending is possible by maintaining the relationship to the fullest even without maxing out your money.

### 3. Criminal Profile

How recklessly or cleanly you operated across Leonida. Each protagonist has a Criminal Profile, visible in the relationship tab of the map menu, tracked across five reputation levels:

1. Professional
2. Hardened
3. Violent
4. Psycho
5. Irredeemable

Stealth takedowns and non-lethal shots build a cleaner profile. Killing builds a worse one, and killing police or civilians can push you to Irredeemable.

### 4. Family savings account

How much money you set aside in the couple's shared savings for a fresh start, versus spending it all along the way. GameSpot reports that the best ending requires the couple to be deeply in love and to have reached the maximum savings total of **$2,000,000**. That figure is based on early reporting and is not yet fully confirmed.

## How to get the best ending (what we know so far)

Based on current reporting, the strongest ending comes from:

- Keeping Jason and Lucia's relationship at its maximum.
- Building the family savings account to $2,000,000.
- Playing clean: favoring stealth and non-lethal approaches to keep your Criminal Profile as Professional as possible.
- Making the kinder narrative choices when the story offers them.

These are early findings. The exact thresholds and how the factors combine are still being worked out by the community.

## Can you see more than one ending?

Yes. Before the final mission, the game asks whether you want to finish the story. After the credits, free roam resumes from just before the finale, with its marker available again, so you can play the finale again for another ending and keep exploring Leonida.

There is a catch: there is no mission replay or chapter select, and GTA 6 has 15 manual save slots. A save from the final mission only takes you back that far. Choices made well before the finale can only be revisited from an earlier save, so keep saves from key turning points if you want to try different outcomes.

## What is still unconfirmed

- The total number of endings.
- Whether the endings are fully distinct or variations on a shared finale.
- The exact savings threshold and how it combines with the other factors.
- Whether Cal and the hard drive, which Rockstar teased with "who knows what will happen," affect a specific ending.
