Heists in GTA 6 are not the board-and-setup missions of GTA 5. There is no planning board where you pick loud or quiet and hire a crew for a cut. Instead, the game has a ladder of robberies, from improvised store stick-ups to planned story heists with a crew, and robbery is a core loop: some story progress requires you to earn cash through robberies before you can continue.

## The robbery ladder

| Tier | Targets | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Small | Convenience stores, gas stations, diners | Rob almost anything, anywhere, in free roam |
| Medium | Jewelry stores, pawn shops, payday loans, armored trucks | Bigger payouts, more security |
| Big | Banks, bank vaults, story heists | The largest payouts and the hardest getaways |

At the small end, a robbery starts because you decide it does. Rockstar North co-head Rob Nelson demonstrated robbing a headphone shop he had simply walked into, noting as the alarm went off that he should have prepared a getaway vehicle first. At the top end sit planned story heists with a crew, a briefing and sometimes a chain of setup missions that change the job itself.

## How a robbery plays

1. **Prepare.** Hide your gun behind your back, wear a mask or face wrap, and park a getaway car outside. Preparation is the difference between a clean job and a disaster.
2. **Control the room.** Aim at the clerk or guard and shout them down so they cannot hit the panic button. Aim at more than one person to keep a standoff under control while your partner works.
3. **Grab the loot.** Safes offer a choice: blow the safe with explosives, which is fast but damages part of the valuables inside, or find the combination, which takes longer but pays far more. A third route shown by Nelson uses a snap gun lock-pick on the back door, then destroying cameras and disabling the alarm to empty the register without anyone ever knowing.
4. **Escape.** Break the police description: change your outfit, ditch the getaway car, switch vehicles. Torching the getaway car and switching rides is shown in the Extended Look. Police remember your face, clothes and car through the heat system.
5. **Cash out.** Stolen goods are not money until a fence buys them. Jewelry, drugs and marked bills each go to the fence who pays best for them.

Vehicle size matters: smaller vehicles hold fewer loot bags, while vans and trucks hold more. Motorcycles are useless for large hauls but excellent for the getaway itself.

## The crew

GTA 5 hired strangers and paid them percentages. GTA 6's default crew is the two protagonists. Your partner is a working part of the job: one character shoots while the other drives, and you can switch between them mid-chase. For the larger jobs there is **Raul Bautista**, described on Rockstar's own character page as a seasoned bank robber hunting for talent willing to take risks. His crew includes Andrea, Nicole, Karim and Felipe.

## Named story heists (reported)

The following planned heists have been reported in launch guides. Details are still being confirmed, so treat payouts as reported figures rather than verified ones.

| Heist | Chapter | Target | Reported payout |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rob Vicki's Boss | 1 | Office safe at Delights, Port Gellhorn | $10,000 cut |
| Twist Wrist, Smash, Grab | 1 | A Gruppe 6 armored van | $2,000 cut |
| The Fleeca Heist (Credit Check) | 3 | Three Fleeca bank branches | $31,860 in marked bills |
| The Cartel Heist | 3 | Sinfrontera National Bank, Vice City | $150,000 |
| Yasmani's Jewelry Jobs | 3 | Three jewelry stores | Not confirmed |
| Mall Jewelry Heist | 4 | Legendary Custom Jewelry, Ocean Beach mall | $12,000 cut |
| VCIA Takedown | 4 | Vice City International Airport | $250,000 |

The Cartel Heist reportedly has 7 setup missions that decide your explosives and time limit, while the VCIA Takedown reportedly has 14. Note that repeatable open-world robberies (stores, jewelry, banks, gang compounds) are a separate system from these planned story heists.

## Heist tips

- **Scout first.** Check security layouts, camera positions and exit routes before starting. The rear exit matters when police gather at the front.
- **Mind the dye packs.** Bank cash carries dye packs until you get the jammer in Chapter 3. See [Fastest Ways to Earn Money Early in GTA 6](/guides/fast-money).
- **Deposit your take.** Cash on your person is lost if you are Wasted or Busted, so bank it at an ATM.
- **Bring the right vehicle.** A fast motorcycle for the escape, then switch to an anonymous car to break the police description.
