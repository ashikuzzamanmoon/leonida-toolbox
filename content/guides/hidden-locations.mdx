GTA 6 launched on November 19, 2026, and the hunt for its hidden places has only just begun. Rockstar has not announced any secret area, and most pre-launch "secret location" lists were guesswork. This guide lists only places that launch-day reporting has actually confirmed, and says plainly what is still unconfirmed. It will grow as players verify more.

For easter eggs and references, see [Easter Eggs & Secrets](/guides/easter-eggs). For the full state layout, see the [Map Guide](/guides/map-guide).

## Confirmed hidden spots

These locations were reported by named outlets from the released game within the first day:

- **The secret ocean stilt house.** Northeast of Corsair Key, out in the Atlantic, a cluster of stilt houses stands in the water. The northernmost house holds dead gang members, scattered cash and cocaine you can take, plus more cash under a loose floorboard, revealed with Focus mode.
- **Manatee Motel.** Southwest of Hamlet, just above the Shoeless Keys, a hidden gang location at a run-down motel. It is one of the game's gang operation sites.
- **South Vice-Dale County warehouse.** Northeast of Hamlet, near the prison where Lucia was held, a warehouse you have to break into holds another gang stash with no enemies to fight.
- **Six shipwrecks around Key Lento.** Sunken Spanish-era wrecks off the Keys hold treasure you can dive for and sell to Kenton Rees, the treasure hunter. You unlock the diving after the Chapter 1 mission Island Dreams. See [Leonida Keys & Islands](/guides/leonida-keys).

## Places worth searching

These are confirmed to exist in the game, even if nobody has published a full list of what they hide yet:

- **Underwater caves.** Rockstar's Extended Look video shows diving inside an underwater cave, with an oxygen meter on the HUD. The seafloor around the Keys has coral reefs, wrecks and marine life including sharks.
- **Grassrivers swamps.** The mangrove channels hide airboat routes, hunting grounds and the Skunk Ape encounter. Take an airboat deep into the wetlands.
- **Mount Kalaga.** The northern forests and cliffs hold hunting spots, meditation spots and off-road trails. See [Hunting & Wildlife](/guides/hunting-wildlife).
- **The Vercetti Estate.** Rockstar's description of the Ultimate Edition revolvers says they were "sourced from the Vercetti Estate," a callback to Tommy Vercetti's mansion from GTA: Vice City. Nobody has confirmed whether the estate itself can be found in the game.

## What is closed off

Not every interesting building opens. At least two large casinos on the map cannot be entered. Rockstar's VP of design Stephen Donkin told Kotaku that gambling "didn't feel like the right place" for Jason and Lucia's story, and that a few areas give Rockstar options in the future. Treat closed buildings as set dressing for now, not as secrets waiting to be opened.

## How to hunt for secrets yourself

- **Use Focus mode** (L3 + R3) to spot hidden details like loose floorboards and stashes.
- **Scan everything with the WAiNK app.** It flags landmarks, sculptures and curiosities, and scanning counts toward 100% completion. See [Collectibles](/guides/collectibles).
- **Dive the Keys.** Scuba gear opens the seafloor, where the shipwreck treasure and underwater discoveries live.
- **Check back here.** New hidden spots are being found daily, and we add them only once a credible source confirms them.
