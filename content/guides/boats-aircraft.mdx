Leonida is built around water: coastline, the Leonida Keys, the Grassrivers swamps and Lake Leonida. Boats are a core part of getting around, not a novelty, and the air fleet covers everything from a crop duster to a fighter jet. One launch-day vehicle list counts 28 boats and 16 aircraft, though counts vary between sources and the full roster is still being mapped.

## Boats

Water gets the biggest expansion of any vehicle category in GTA 6. Confirmed boats include:

- **Shitzu Squalo** — the classic speedboat, back from GTA 5. An Ultimate Edition version is moored at Washington Beach from Chapter 4.
- **Pegassi Speeder** — another returning speedboat, seen in Trailer 1.
- **Dinka Marquis** — a sailing yacht, confirmed returning.
- **Nagasaki Dinghy** — the small inflatable, confirmed returning.
- **Shitzu Tropic** — a cabin cruiser.
- **Speedophile Seashark** — the jet ski, seen in sunset convoys off the beach.
- **Airboat 09** — a genuinely new vehicle type, a fanboat built for the shallow swamps of Grassrivers. A POACH-marked variant is also confirmed.
- **Crest Kayak** — a kayak, included with the Ultimate Edition at Jason's house in Key Lento.
- **Lure Predator** — a fishing boat, with an S23 variant also spotted.

Larger vessels such as a cruise ship, yachts, a shrimp boat and a dive boat appear in the world, but most are scenery rather than drivable craft. Whether any large yacht is player-drivable is unconfirmed.

Boats matter for gameplay too: treasure diving with Kenton in Key Lento needs a boat and scuba gear, and several gang operations and hidden spots are only reachable by water.

## Helicopters

Confirmed helicopters include:

- **Buckingham Maverick** — the standard civilian helicopter. One waits at the Southwind dock near the Dodo pickup.
- **Nagasaki Buzzard** — the attack helicopter, back with its weapons.
- **Nagasaki Sea Sparrow** — the small amphibious helicopter, featured on the game's cover art.
- **Buckingham Police Maverick (Polmav)** — police air support during pursuits.
- **Buckingham Supervolito, Volatus and Valkyrie** — larger and military helicopters also confirmed.

Helicopters are the fastest way to cross the whole state, and police helicopters with searchlights are part of the pursuit response at higher wanted levels.

## Planes

Confirmed planes include:

- **Mammoth Dodo** — the seaplane, modeled on the de Havilland Beaver.
- **Buckingham Shamal** — the private jet.
- **Western Duster** — the crop duster biplane.
- **Lapwing Mallard** — a stunt plane.
- **Jobuilt Mammatus** — a small prop plane.
- **Hauners P-996 Lazer** — the fighter jet.
- **A four-engine jumbo jet** based on the Boeing 747-400 appears in official material, but whether it is flyable is unconfirmed. Treat that claim with caution.

A blimp and a tethered radar aerostat have also been spotted in the skies over Leonida.

## Getting around by water and air

- **The Keys are boat country.** The Leonida Keys are a chain of islands where a boat is often faster than driving. See [Leonida Keys](/guides/leonida-keys).
- **Swamps need airboats.** Normal boats cannot handle the shallow Grassrivers wetlands; the airboat is built for them.
- **Aircraft are endgame toys.** Most planes and helicopters are found at airfields and docks, and stealing a military aircraft will draw a heavy police response.
