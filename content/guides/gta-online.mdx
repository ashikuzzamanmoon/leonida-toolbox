There is no online mode in GTA 6 at launch. Rockstar has not announced a new GTA Online, given it a name, or dated it. This guide covers what is confirmed, what is only reported, and what is still unknown.

## Confirmed: GTA 6 launches as a single-player game

Rockstar's pre-order announcement of June 24, 2026 describes Grand Theft Auto VI as a single-player experience, and both console store listings say the same. PlayStation's store page lists the game as offline play only for one player, and Microsoft's store lists exactly one play capability: single player.

Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick repeated the point at the company's shareholder meeting on September 17, 2026, describing the launch as a single-player title. Rockstar has not announced an online mode for launch day or for any other day.

## What is not announced

As of November 20, 2026, there is no official information on:

- **A name** for any new online mode.
- **A release date or window.**
- **Features, player count or price.**
- **Crossplay or cross-progression** between PS5 and Xbox.
- **Whether progress or purchases from the existing GTA Online carry over.** Take-Two has said it will keep supporting the existing GTA Online after GTA 6 launches, but has said nothing about transfers.

Any site claiming a day-one online mode, a feature list or a price is going beyond the confirmed record.

## Reported: multiplayer in 2027

The strongest hints come from reporting, not announcements:

- **Bloomberg** reported in October 2026 that the online component is expected to be released in 2027.
- **Twitch CEO Dan Clancy** told Bloomberg in September 2026 that Twitch had spent a lot of time talking with Rockstar about its plans, and predicted an even bigger viewership moment "next year when they launch multiplayer." That is his expectation, not a Rockstar date.
- **Jason Schreier** has reported that Take-Two is not planning to talk about the new GTA Online any time soon, and is only discussing GTA 6 in terms of its single-player portion.

A weaker, single-sourced claim points to mid-December 2026, about a month after launch, with no corroboration. Treat it as a rumor.

## The one official hint: a 32-player format

The only place Rockstar has mentioned an online format in writing is an Edinburgh employment tribunal, reported in September 2026, where company documents listed "an online 32 player format" among confidential features. That confirms an online format exists inside the company. It says nothing about when it launches, what it contains, or whether it is the final form.

## What happened last time

GTA Online followed GTA 5 by two weeks in 2013, but Rockstar announced that stagger in advance. No equivalent announcement exists for GTA 6, so the two-week precedent is not a reliable guide to timing here.

## What to do in the meantime

The single-player game is enormous on its own: reviewers took 60 to 80 hours to finish the story, with around 200 hours estimated for full completion. See [How Long to Beat GTA 6](/guides/how-long-to-beat). When an online mode is announced, it will appear on Rockstar's Newswire and the official GTA 6 page first, and we will update this guide the same day.

## Watch out for scams

With no official online mode, fake "GTA 6 Online" downloads, early access offers and beta invites are circulating. Rockstar has announced no beta and no early access. Do not download anything claiming to be GTA 6 Online, and do not enter your account details on unofficial pages.
