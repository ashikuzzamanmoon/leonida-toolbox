GTA 6 limits what you can carry: two sidearms and two longarms, plus throwables. With only four gun slots, the right question is which weapons cover different situations without doubling up. Rockstar has not published damage or fire-rate figures, and no tested damage ranking exists yet, so the picks below are early editorial recommendations based on shop ratings, roles and availability. Expect them to change as weapons are tested.

## The picks in detail

**Duke 556 — best all-round weapon.** The M4-style carbine rifle is the only assault rifle available early, and the sensible default for most fights. Its shop ratings are 5.0 damage, 6.0 accuracy and 6.2 range, with a 30-round magazine and a good range of attachments. It requires a bank account to buy.

**Klose K17 and Girardi ES9 — best sidearms.** These are the personal pistols of Lucia and Jason respectively, named in the Ultimate Edition: the Klose K17 is Lucia's service pistol and the Girardi ES9 is Jason's. The K17 has a 17-round magazine and a handling rating of 8.5.

**Moreland 850 — best close-range gun.** The Fortune & Son pump-action shotgun fires up to 5 shots before reloading and is the reliable choice for indoor fights and room clearing.

**Laird 350 — best early long-range weapon.** The only purchasable early-game sniper, and very affordable at $950.

**Vom Feuer VF5 — best SMG.** The military SMG is the pick for close-to-medium range, where its fire rate matters more than precision.

**Hawk & Little Morgan — best revolver.** A scoped revolver named in the Ultimate Edition, for precise single shots. The Duke Mustang .357 is the cheaper revolver alternative at $1,800.

**Hillinger Boomer — heavy weapon.** The compact grenade launcher is a specialist purchase at $12,000, for vehicles and groups rather than everyday use.

**Baseball Bat — best melee.** Accessible early (it can be found at the Pay 'n' Spray during the Unwanted Attention mission) with long reach.

## Loadout advice

- **Cover three ranges.** A rifle, a sidearm and a shotgun cover almost every fight. Add the sniper or SMG as your fourth gun depending on the job.
- **Buy, don't just pick up.** Only weapons bought at Ammu-Nation count as owned, and only owned weapons can be modified or recovered from your car trunk or a weapon locker.
- **Carry armor.** Body armor is bought at Ammu-Nation and makes a bigger difference than a marginal weapon upgrade.
