Cheat codes are back in GTA 6, but Rockstar has changed how they work. There are **61 cheats** in the game, and every one of them is locked behind the main story and its own unlock challenge. You cannot use a single cheat until the credits roll, and many of the codes themselves are still being discovered by players. This guide covers the system, the penalties, and the codes that multiple launch-day guides agree on. We will not list a code here unless at least two independent sources report the same input.

## How cheats work

- **Finish the story first.** No cheat can be used until you complete the final mission. Entering a known code before then does nothing.
- **Each cheat has an unlock requirement.** Some unlock just by finishing the story. Others need a challenge, such as winning shooting range medals, completing stunt jumps, or spending money on alcohol. You can complete these challenges during the story; you just cannot use the cheat until the story is done.
- **The Cheats list appears after the credits.** Once the story is finished, a Cheats list shows up under Progress > Cheats, showing which cheats you have unlocked and which challenges are still open.

## How to enter a cheat

Cheats are entered through your in-game phone with a D-pad sequence. The sequences are identical on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

1. Press **Up on the D-pad** to bring up your phone.
2. Hold **L1 (PS5) or LB (Xbox)** and press **R1 or RB**.
3. Enter the cheat's D-pad sequence.

## The penalties

Every cheat carries two costs, so use them on a separate session from your serious play:

- **Trophies and achievements are disabled** while a cheat is active.
- **Saving is blocked** while a cheat is active.
- Cheats work in single-player story mode only. There is no multiplayer at launch to use them in.

## Cheats still being confirmed

At launch, the inputs for only 17 of the 61 cheats were public. The rest are listed in the game's Cheats menu with their unlock requirements, but their D-pad sequences are still undiscovered. Known unlocks whose codes are not yet verified include:

- **Infinite Ammo:** win 25 silver medals or higher at shooting ranges.
- **No Reload:** win 25 gold medals at shooting ranges.
- **Parachute:** complete 50 skydives or base jumps from 300 feet or higher.
- **Unlimited Nitrous:** complete 50 stunt jumps.
- **Move Time Forward:** find 10 collectible watches.
- **Pause Time of Day:** find 20 collectible watches.
- **Vehicle spawns** for the Bravado Banshee, Airboat 09 and others, each tied to its own challenge.
