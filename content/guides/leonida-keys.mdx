The Leonida Keys are a chain of tropical islands off the southern tip of Leonida, GTA 6's version of the Florida Keys. Rockstar calls the region a gateway to paradise, with a casual dress code and loaded bars. It is also where the story begins: Jason Duval lives here, and roughly the first fifth of the story plays out in the Keys before the action moves to Vice City.

The Keys sit in Mariana County, linked by the Keys Causeway and a string of bridges over turquoise water. For the wider state, see the [Map Guide](/guides/map-guide).

## Key Lento

Key Lento is the main island and the place you will know best. It is modeled on Key Largo, the northernmost and largest of the real Florida Keys. The name is a musical joke: largo and lento are both tempo markings meaning slow.

Key places on Key Lento:

- **Jason's house.** A green stilt house on the shore, owned by smuggler Brian Heder, who lets Jason live there rent-free in exchange for help with local shakedowns. It is your first [safehouse](/guides/safehouses). Lucia moves in later.
- **Brian's Boat Works and Marina.** Brian Heder's boatyard, the front for his smuggling operation, run with his wife Lori.
- **The Rusty Anchor.** The bar in the northwest corner of the island, with a pool opponent and an arcade game inside.
- **Kenton Rees's shop.** Home base for the treasure diving side content.

The story's opening mission, Gone Fishing, takes place here, and several Chapter 1 collectibles sit in and around Jason's house. Pick them up before the end of Chapter 1.

## The other islands

The official map names several more islands along the chain, from north to south:

- **Corsair Key**, connected to Key Lento by bridge.
- **Abismo Key** and the **Shoeless Keys**, further down the chain.
- **Goose Key**, home to the settlement of Southwind.
- **Mile Zero Air Station**, at the southern end of the chain.

Rockstar has not published a full island count, and other names circulating online from fan-made maps are unconfirmed.

## What to do in the Keys

The Keys run on water and quiet work: fishing, seaside bars, boating, and smuggling that stays out of sight.

- **Shipwreck treasure diving.** After the Chapter 1 mission Island Dreams, treasure hunter Kenton Rees sends you to six Spanish-era wrecks around Key Lento, including the Nuestra Senora de la Noche. Each wreck holds valuables you can sell to Kenton. There are 7 treasure sets in total and 33 underwater discoveries. See [Collectibles](/guides/collectibles).
- **Scuba diving and snorkeling.** The reefs around the Keys hold coral, sea turtles, rays and sharks. An oxygen meter appears on the HUD while diving.
- **Fishing.** The Keys are a good early spot for the game's catch-and-release fishing, with 35 fish species across fresh and salt water.
- **Boating and jet skiing.** The fastest way between islands, and the only way to reach some spots, including a secret stilt house northeast of Corsair Key. See [Hidden Locations](/guides/hidden-locations).
- **Wildlife.** Iguanas, pelicans, dolphins, sea turtles and rays all live in and around the Keys. Study them with binoculars for the animal log. See [Hunting & Wildlife](/guides/hunting-wildlife).

## Getting to the mainland

The Keys Causeway connects the chain to the mainland through Grassrivers. The drive north passes Cottonmouth Lake and the swamplands where the Skunk Ape roams. See [Easter Eggs](/guides/easter-eggs). Once the story moves on, you can return to the Keys any time by road, boat or air.
