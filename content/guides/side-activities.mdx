Beyond the main story, Leonida is packed with things to do. Launch guides count more than 40 side activities, from full sports to water activities, racing and crime. Many count toward 100% completion, so they are worth doing even if you are mainly here for the story.

## Sports and games

- **Basketball.** Street-court shooting with manually aimed and timed shots. Good streaks can top up your Focus. There are 100 hoops across Leonida, 90 of which count toward 100% completion.
- **Golf.** Full-course golf on two courses: Leaf Links in Vice Beach and the Mount Hailes Club in Leonard County.
- **Mini golf.** A round of putt-putt for a low-stakes break.
- **Pool and darts.** Found in dive bars around Leonida, including in Port Gellhorn.
- **Gym.** Lifting weights visibly changes Jason and Lucia's physiques over time, and what you eat affects how they look too. Lucia is shown working out with button prompts in the Extended Look.
- **Shooting range.** Ammu-Nation ranges run timed courses against 12 targets, with time bonuses for hits.

## Water activities

- **Fishing.** A returning mechanic from Red Dead Redemption 2, spotted in official images of Jason fishing with Cal. Spots exist along the coast, rivers and lakes.
- **Scuba diving.** Dive with a wetsuit among manta rays, explore underwater caves and shipwrecks, and hunt treasure with Kenton in Key Lento.
- **Kayaking and canoeing.** Paddle the ocean and the inland waterways.
- **Jet skiing.** Ride the Seashark off the beaches.
- **Airboating.** Fanboats built for the shallow, alligator-filled waters of Grassrivers.
- **Swimming and sunbathing.** Swim anywhere, with dive tricks such as front flips, or tan on the beach.

## Racing

- **Street races, dirt bike races and rally races** all appear as activities, with 40 unique race locations in total.
- **Gellhorn International Raceway** in Port Gellhorn is the series' first full-size circuit.
- **Supercar racing** takes place on dedicated tracks.

## Crime as a side activity

Robbery is a repeatable open-world activity in GTA 6, not just story content. See [Heists in GTA 6: What We Know](/guides/heists) for the full picture.

- **Gang operations.** 39 gang-run sites across Leonida, belonging to nine groups, holding narcotics, watches and safes.
- **Store stick-ups.** 91 stores can be robbed; 50 count toward 100% completion.
- **Armored truck robberies.** Hold up an armored truck in the open world. Getting away earns the A Classic Blitz Play trophy.
- **Bank jobs and vaults.** 22 bank jobs plus 8 vaults at the larger banks. Robbing five vaults earns the Maximum Withdrawal trophy.
- **Payday loan jobs.** 7 in total, all counting toward 100% completion.

## Leisure and nightlife

- **Nightclubs and strip clubs.** Club 919 in Downtown Vice City and Boobie Ike's Jack of Hearts are key nightlife spots, and clubs can trigger side content.
- **Watching TV.** In-game shows and commercials play at home, including parody ads and nature documentaries.
- **VR.** A virtual reality headset was shown in use in the Extended Look.
- **Zoos.** Confirmed by Rockstar as a place to blow off steam, fitting for a map with over 170 animal species, including manatees, flamingos, pelicans and alligators.
- **Wildlife watching and hunting.** Over 60 bird types and more than 30 land mammals roam Leonida. See [Hunting & Wildlife](/guides/hunting-wildlife).
- **Base jumping and skydiving.** Leap from tall buildings and open the parachute over the city.
- **Backyard wrestling and cage fighting.** Hand-to-hand combat as a pastime.
- **Amusement parks.** Funfair rides are among the confirmed activities.

## Getting around for fun

- **Electric scooters.** Rentable e-scooters for eco-friendly city travel.
- **Cycling.** BMX and mountain bikes are in the game for tricks and trail riding.
