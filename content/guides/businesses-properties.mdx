If you played GTA 5 expecting to buy businesses that pay weekly income, GTA 6 works differently. Garages are the only property you can buy. Safehouses come with the story, and every business in Leonida already belongs to someone else. No passive-income business has been found in story mode so far.

## Garages: the one property you can buy

You start with room for three personal vehicles. Once those slots are full, you either sell a car or buy a garage. There are 8 purchasable garages, bought through the Vice City Real Estate app on your phone, which is installed after the Chapter 2 mission Daily Driver.

- All 8 garages together cost **$160,000**.
- The cheapest is Mitchell Auto Center in Kelly County at **$4,000**.
- The most expensive is Atlantic Court Garage in Ocean Beach at **$40,000**.
- Each garage adds parking spots for registered vehicles. Some also include a weapon locker and a drop-off point for stolen goods.
- Owning all 8 unlocks the **Little Boxes** trophy/achievement.

Four more garages are given free by the story as you complete main missions, and three single-car garages come with the pre-order bonus, the Ultimate Edition and GTA+ respectively. Each of those comes with its own car.

## Safehouses: story-given, not for sale

Jason and Lucia live where the story puts them. Rockstar has said buying homes would not fit two characters who are always on the run, so safehouses unlock as their living situation changes across the chapters. Rockstar compares them to the camps in Red Dead Redemption 2: interactive places that feel like home, with daily routines, a stocked fridge and a safe zone around them where you cannot steal cars.

Jason starts in a house in the Leonida Keys owned by Brian Heder, who lets him stay rent-free in exchange for help with his business. Safehouses don't add registered garage spots, but you can leave a stolen car in the parking spot outside your current safehouse to keep it there. To make a car properly yours, register it and store it in a garage. See [Safehouses](/guides/safehouses) for the full list.

## Businesses: all owned by someone else

Leonida is full of businesses, but the ones Rockstar names all belong to story characters:

- **Brian's Boat Works & Marina** in the Leonida Keys, run by Brian Heder.
- **Jack of Hearts** strip club, part of Boobie Ike's empire of real estate, clubs and a recording studio.
- **Only Raw Records**, Boobie Ike and Dre'Quan Priest's record label, home to the rap duo Real Dimez.

None of these can be bought, taken over or run by the player. No preview, trailer or Rockstar page has shown a purchasable business, and nothing found in the game since launch has changed that. The Ultimate Edition does open five shops (Rideout Customs, One-Eyed Willie's, Sara's Unisex Salon, Stock 305 and the Electric Fang tattoo parlour), but you shop there, you don't own them.

## What to spend on instead

Since there is no passive income to invest in, your money goes further on:

- **Garages**, so you can keep more registered vehicles.
- **Weapons and attachments** at Ammu-Nation. See [Best Weapons in GTA 6](/guides/best-weapons).
- **Vehicle upgrades** at mod shops. See [Vehicle Customization in GTA 6](/guides/vehicle-customization).
- **Family Savings**, the "Save for the Future" goal, which costs $2,000,000 to max out.

For how to earn the cash in the first place, see [How to Make Money in GTA 6](/guides/money-guide).
