GTA 6 takes place in Leonida, Rockstar's fictional version of Florida, nicknamed "The Goodtime State." It stretches from the tropical island chain of the Leonida Keys in the south to the forests of Mount Kalaga National Park on the northern border. On launch day, November 19, 2026, Rockstar published a free printable map of the state, confirming the layout players had pieced together from trailers.

This guide explains how Leonida is put together: its six regions, its five counties, and how to get around. For a zoomable version, use our [interactive map](/map).

## The six regions

Rockstar's official site profiles six regions. These are the big geographic identities of the state:

- **Vice City.** The metropolis on the southeast coast, GTA 6's main city. See the [Vice City Guide](/guides/vice-city).
- **Leonida Keys.** The laid-back tropical island chain at the southern tip, connected by causeway bridges. The story starts here. See [Leonida Keys & Islands](/guides/leonida-keys).
- **Grassrivers.** Untamed mangrove wetlands southwest of Vice City, full of alligators and airboats. It is the Everglades of Leonida.
- **Port Gellhorn.** A faded resort town on the west coast, described by Rockstar as "Leonida's forgotten coast," full of cheap motels and empty strip malls.
- **Ambrosia.** The inland industrial heartland, built around a giant sugar refinery near Lake Leonida.
- **Mount Kalaga National Park.** Forested hills on the northern border, with hunting, fishing and off-road trails. See [Hunting & Wildlife](/guides/hunting-wildlife).

## The five counties

The official printable map divides Leonida into five counties, which are the labels you will see on road signs and in the game's location names:

| County | Where it is | Places inside it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lummox County | The north | Mount Kalaga National Park, Grayson, Bonanza, Millport |
| Leonard County | The northeast | Lake Leonida, Belville, Waning Sands, Vinewood Beach |
| Kelly County | The west and center | Port Gellhorn, Emerald Springs, Buckskin Swamp, Capri |
| Vice-Dale County | The southeast | Vice City, Vice Beach, Port Vice City, Vice City International Airport |
| Mariana County | The south and the Keys | Grassrivers, Watson Bay, Pelican Valley, Key Lento, Southwind |

Ambrosia sits between Kelly and Leonard counties, just west of Lake Leonida. The town of Hamlet sits on the southern edge of the mainland, between Grassrivers and Vice City.

## How big is Leonida?

Leonida is the largest map Rockstar has ever built. Reporting on Game Informer's cover story puts it at roughly twice the size of GTA V's map, and the state is dense: the six regions pack in a major city, an island chain, wetlands, farmland, mountains and dozens of small towns. Expect long drives between regions, especially from the Keys in the south to Mount Kalaga in the north.

## Getting around

There is no traditional fast travel menu in GTA 6, but you have options:

- **RydeMe app.** Open your phone, pick a destination in the RydeMe app, and a car takes you there for a fee. You need an active waypoint.
- **Autopilot.** Hold Down on the D-pad to enter Cinematic Camera mode, which drives for you along the route to your waypoint. If your partner is in the car, you can also ask them to drive.
- **Boats and aircraft.** The Keys and the coast are often faster by water or air than by road, and some spots are only reachable that way.
- **On foot in the wilds.** The swamps and mountains reward slow exploration. Airboats in Grassrivers and off-road vehicles in Mount Kalaga open up areas cars cannot reach.
