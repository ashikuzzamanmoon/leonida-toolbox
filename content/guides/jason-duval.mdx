Jason Duval is one of GTA 6's two playable protagonists, alongside Lucia Caminos, and the character you spend the opening hours with. This guide covers his backstory, his skills and how he plays.

## Backstory

Jason grew up around grifters and crooks. After a stint in the Army trying to shake off his troubled teens, he found himself in the Leonida Keys doing what he knows best: working for local drug runners.

He did not leave the Army on the best of terms. In the launch trailer he says he got in trouble during his service, and Rockstar has not said which unit he served in. Back in civilian life, he fell in with Brian Heder, a veteran smuggler who lets Jason live rent-free in one of his waterfront properties in Key Lento in exchange for muscle work, boat runs and the occasional shakedown.

He wants an easy life, but things just keep getting harder. Rockstar presents meeting Lucia as the turning point of his life, one that could be the best or the worst thing that ever happens to him.

## Personality

Jason is laid-back and easygoing, loyal to the people close to him, and street-smart. He is the instinctive half of the duo: where Lucia plans, Jason acts. He is also a natural wheelman and a steady hand in a firefight, with the composure of someone who has been in trouble before and come out of it.

## Skills and abilities

- **Marksmanship.** Army training gave him solid firearms fundamentals, and he favors pistols and shotguns.
- **Driving and boating.** An exceptional driver and boat pilot, at home threading a boat through the Keys' shallow channels or losing cops on the highway.
- **Hand-to-hand combat.** A strong, athletic fighter who trains with heavy weights.
- **Sidearm.** His pistol of choice is a Girardi ES9. The Ultimate Edition adds a personalized, engraved version.
- **Focus mode.** Gameplay footage shows Jason slowing time to aim during a shootout, and focus also highlights loot while exploring.

## Jason in the story

**Light spoilers for the opening follow.** After Lucia's prologue, the story shifts to Jason in Key Lento. He works for Brian Heder while renovating the house Brian provided, preparing for Lucia's release and their new start together. His best friend Cal Hampton is his partner in most of the early jobs.

His starting vehicle is a '70 Vapid Ganado pickup, and one of the first missions has him take it to Coastal Auto Care in Key Lento for a free engine repair and respray. He also keeps a pet snake in a terrarium near his kitchen, which you can feed to raise your focus stat. For the full plot, see [Story Explained](/guides/story-explained).

## Playing as Jason

Jason is your only playable character in the open world for the first hours, after the Gone Fishing mission. Lucia becomes playable in the open world after Chapter 1 ends, and free switching unlocks in Chapter 2 after the Breakfast Run mission. See [How Character Switching Works](/guides/character-switching).

A few things to know when playing as Jason:

- **Some missions are Jason only.** Bret Vogel's hunting chain, Pilot Pete's plane recovery and two of Yago's races require him.
- **His house is your early base.** Stash cash under the loose floorboards in his bedroom, check the mailbox outside for mission payments, and feed the snake for focus.
- **Ultimate Edition extras.** Jason's Safehouse Vehicles include an army fatigue Dinka Enduro motorcycle and a Crest Kayak, and the Ganado Retro Build adds exclusive mods for his pickup.

## Relationships

- **Lucia Caminos:** his partner in crime and in life. Their relationship is a central system in the game and shapes the ending. See the [Endings Guide](/guides/endings).
- **Brian Heder:** the local dealer Jason works for, who provides his house. Voiced by Stephen Root, the first major cast member confirmed for the game.
- **Cal Hampton:** Jason's best friend and partner in the early jobs.
