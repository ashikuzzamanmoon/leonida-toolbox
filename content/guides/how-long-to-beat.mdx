GTA 6 is the longest story Rockstar has ever made. Rockstar has not published an official runtime, but review playthroughs from the November 18 embargo and comments from Rockstar North give a clear picture.

## The short answer

| Play style | Estimated time |
| --- | --- |
| Story only, rushing | 45 to 55 hours |
| Typical story playthrough | 60 to 80 hours |
| Story plus a lot of side content | 100 to 150 hours |
| 100% completion / Platinum | Around 200 hours |

## Story length: what reviewers reported

Reviewers who finished the campaign reported a wide range, because how much side content you do changes the answer by dozens of hours:

- **Press Start** finished just under 50 hours, the fastest credible story time.
- **Screen Rant** finished in 50 hours 42 minutes while skipping most side content.
- **Push Square** finished the story in about 60 hours, and about 80 with side content.
- **Eurogamer** finished in 65 hours, pushing hard on story missions near the end.
- **PSU** took around 80 hours for the main story.
- **Wccftech** reviewed the game after about 90 hours.
- **IGN's** two reviewers logged around 70 and 115 hours with side content included.

Most reviewers landed between 60 and 80 hours for a normal playthrough, and Polygon advises expecting 70 to 80 unless you mainline the missions.

## What Rockstar says

Rockstar North co-studio head Rob Nelson has given two figures. He told The New York Times that a playthrough in February 2026 took about 80 hours, including optional goals with story consequences. Closer to launch, he told Polygon his latest story run took just over 100 hours, in a playthrough he described as rushed. Neither figure is an official runtime, but they line up with the reviewer range.

## How the story is structured

The campaign runs across a prologue and five chapters, with 91 to 93 story missions depending on how optional jobs are counted. Chapters 3 and 4 are the longest by far, with 26 and 25 missions, while the final chapter has only seven. See the [Story Mission List](/guides/story-missions) for the full breakdown.

## 100% completion and the Platinum

Full completion is a much bigger project. Estimates cluster around 200 hours, though nobody has reported finishing it yet. Windows Central and Insider Gaming both put it near 200 hours, while Push Square estimates more than 300 hours for everything. The 100% checklist only opens after the story, and early reports describe a long list that includes dozens of challenges, stunts, basketball hoops and vehicle scans.

Some trophies may also depend partly on luck: Insider Gaming reported that some GTA 6 trophies could be RNG-based. See [Trophies and Achievements](/guides/trophies-achievements) for what is known so far.

## How it compares to earlier games

GTA 6 roughly doubles the length of its predecessors. GTA 5's story takes about 32 hours, GTA 4's about 27, and Red Dead Redemption 2's about 50. If you booked a week off for launch, the story alone will fill most of it.
