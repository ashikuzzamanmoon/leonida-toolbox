Leonida is huge and GTA 6 hides many of its most useful systems until you stumble into them. These tips come from Rockstar developers and from post-launch guides, and they are all spoiler-free. For first steps, see the [Beginner's Guide](/guides/beginners-guide) and [First Things to Do](/guides/first-things-to-do).

## Money

- **Bank your cash at an ATM.** Rockstar's Rob Nelson confirmed that the cash you carry disappears if you are killed before you deposit it. Use an ATM after every job and before anything risky.
- **Scan a car before you steal it.** Your phone can scan a vehicle and show whether it is locked, has an alarm or a tracker, and what it is worth at a Pay 'n' Spray or a car fence.
- **Serious money starts in Chapter 3.** Early on, money is tight by design. Once you reach Chapter 3, prioritize Jason's missions: establishing connections with fences Tyrone and Yasmani opens up far better ways to earn.
- **Sell what you find.** Collectible cards can be sold at pawn shops without losing collection progress, and sunken treasure sells to Kenton Rees in the Keys.

## The police

- **No witness, no police.** A crime is only reported if someone sees it or an alarm goes off. A quiet job in a side street is far safer than the same job in a crowd.
- **Hollow stars mean they don't know who you are.** At a one-star wanted level, leave the area before the police arrive and don't escalate.
- **Change your clothes, not just your hair.** The police remember what you are wearing. Duck into a clothing store, or use the spare outfits kept in your personal vehicle's trunk.
- **Ditch the car and split up.** If the police know what you are driving, get out of it. Jason and Lucia splitting up also lowers what the police know about you.

## Your partner

- **Hold Right on the D-pad** to issue contextual commands to your partner when you are together: ask them to distract the cops, take the wheel on a long drive, or sell stolen stash for you.
- **Jason and Lucia have separate inventories.** Check who is carrying what before a job, and keep both of them stocked.
- **Spend time together.** Activities, exploring and even robberies together keep the relationship strong, and it affects how the pair work as a team.

## Getting around

- **There is no traditional fast travel, but the RydeMe app is close.** Open your phone, set a waypoint, and a car takes you there for a fee.
- **Use autopilot.** Hold Down on the D-pad to enter Cinematic Camera mode and the car drives itself to your waypoint. Or ask your partner to drive.
- **Steal cars faster.** When a break-in minigame appears, for a Slim Jim, a Clone Key or hotwiring, there is a pink sweet spot on the dial. Find it to speed up the job.

## Settings worth changing

- **Controls.** The default scheme maps run to L3 and jump to Cross/X. If a decade of GTA V muscle memory fights you, switch to the Classic scheme under Settings > Control Scheme.
- **Difficulty.** There is no traditional difficulty setting, but Settings > Gameplay has Accessible Combat (less damage taken, more damage dealt) and Reduced Vehicle Damage.
- **Minimap.** If the minimap fading in and out annoys you, the settings let you lock it on screen.
- **Units.** Set distance, temperature and weight to the units you think in, so you stop doing mental conversions.

## Saving

- **Save in a fresh slot at the start of each chapter.** There is no mission replay menu, so a manual save is the only way to replay a finished mission or retry a big choice. You get 15 manual slots. See [Saves & Cross-Progression](/guides/saves-cross-progression).
- **Save before you stop playing.** Autosave only fires when a mission ends, so anything you did after the last mission is lost if the game crashes.
