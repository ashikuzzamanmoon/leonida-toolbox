Weapon customization in GTA 6 happens at Ammu-Nation, and it goes further than in GTA 5: more attachment slots, cosmetic finishes, and a loyalty system where buying more unlocks more stock. The key rule is that only guns you own can be modified.

## Owned vs unowned weapons

GTA 6 draws a hard line between weapons you own and weapons you merely picked up:

- **Owned weapons** are bought at Ammu-Nation. They can be modified, and if you drop or lose one, you can recover it from your personal vehicle's trunk or a weapon locker. Both Jason and Lucia can access owned weapons, but each character's modifications and equipped loadout are separate.
- **Unowned weapons** are picked up from the ground or taken from enemies. You can use them, but you cannot modify them, and you lose them when you drop them.
- **Unique weapons** are one-of-a-kind guns found in the world. Both characters can use them once picked up, but they cannot be modified either.

You can carry two sidearms and two longarms at a time. Everything else lives in your car trunk or an Ammu-Nation locker.

## Where to buy and modify

Ammu-Nation is the gun store chain across Leonida, with 24 locations. Your first visit comes early in Chapter 1 during the mission Pick Up Another Gun, when Brian sends Jason to Chuck's Tackle and Shot. Buying and modifying properly unlocks during the Chapter 1 mission Gear Up.

Not every store stocks the same weapons. Larger Ammu-Nation stores carry more guns and more customization options than small ones, so shop around if you can't find a specific weapon or part. Stores also sell ammunition, body armor and explosives, which are delivered automatically to your car's trunk.

**Loyalty unlocks:** the more you spend at Ammu-Nation, the more products unlock for purchase, including extra attachments. Buying more guns opens up larger stores and bigger inventories.

## Attachment slots

To modify a weapon, visit Ammu-Nation, select a gun you own and open the Modify menu, then the Attachments tab. Not every weapon supports every attachment. The main categories are:

- **Barrels** — improve ranged damage and long-distance performance.
- **Grips and foregrips** — improve handling and control.
- **Magazines** — extended magazines and drum mags for more rounds before reloading.
- **Optics** — iron sights, scopes and holographic sights for better aiming.
- **Muzzles and suppressors** — suppressors quieten the weapon, which helps with stealth approaches. They are inexpensive early in the game.
- **Tactical attachments** — weapon lights for dark interiors and night work.

Every attachment costs extra money on top of the gun itself.

## Field swaps

Some attachments can be changed while you are out in the world. Suppressors and flashlights can be taken off or put on during exploration, so you can go quiet for a stealth approach and loud again after. More permanent attachments, such as barrels and grips, can only be changed back at an Ammu-Nation.

## Cosmetics: palettes, colors and wraps

Looks are handled in the Cosmetics menu at Ammu-Nation, with three categories: Palettes (preset color combinations), Colors (custom color choices) and Wraps (patterns and finishes). The Vintage Vice City Pack pre-order bonus adds a weapon pattern, the first confirmed weapon finish in the game.

## Managing your arsenal

- **Weapon lockers** at every Ammu-Nation let you swap your loadout before a job and switch between standard and alternate variants of many weapons.
- **Your car trunk** holds your owned guns. You can call your personal vehicle to you at any time, and a delivery service brings it over with your arsenal inside.
- **The weapon wheel** is redesigned with separate Weapons and Items tabs, flipped with the shoulder button. The Items tab covers body armor, healing items and gear.
