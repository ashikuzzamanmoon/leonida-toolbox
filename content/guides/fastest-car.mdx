**The Grotti Itali RSX is the fastest car in GTA 6 on current estimates**, with a top speed of around 135 mph (217 km/h) without engine upgrades. The fastest car in the dedicated Super class is the Pegassi Tempesta at around 128 mph (206 km/h).

Read that with a caveat: Rockstar has not published official top speeds for any GTA 6 vehicle, and the game only launched on November 19, 2026. Nobody has had time to properly test every car yet. Every number on this page is an estimate from community databases, and the ranking will change as cars are timed in-game.

## Top 5 fastest cars (estimated)

| Rank | Car | Class | Estimated top speed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Grotti Itali RSX | Sports | ~135 mph / 217 km/h |
| 2 | Pegassi Tempesta | Super | ~128 mph / 206 km/h |
| 3 | Pegassi Toros | SUV | ~127 mph / 204 km/h |
| 4 | Progen Emerus | Super | ~126 mph / 203 km/h |
| 5 | Pegassi Zorrusso | Super | ~125 mph / 201 km/h |

The Grotti Furia and Truffade Thrax sit just behind this group, both estimated above 120 mph. Note that some sources rank the Furia first on paper, based on its real-world counterpart. Until cars are timed on the same stretch of road in-game, the order below the Itali RSX is genuinely uncertain.

## Why the estimates differ

Different guides use different methods, which is why you will see conflicting lists:

- **GTA Online data.** Returning cars are often ranked by their GTA Online speeds, but GTA 6 rebalances vehicles with its own handling data, so those numbers are a rough guide at best.
- **Real-world counterparts.** Some rankings use the top speed of the real car each GTA car is based on. The Thrax's Bugatti Divo basis is limited to 380 km/h in real life, which would make it fastest on paper, but that says nothing about its in-game tuning.
- **Early in-game testing.** A few outlets have begun timing cars, but coverage is still patchy and no full tested list exists yet.

## How to get the fastest cars

Speed is only half the problem. Almost every supercar in GTA 6 carries a tracker, and advanced trackers cannot be removed by Moses or Pay 'n' Spray shops until later in the story. Even if you steal a Tempesta early, you cannot register it as your own until you can deal with the tracker.

- **Scan with WAINK first.** The phone app shows a car's tracker status and registration cost before you steal it.
- **Progress the story.** Tracker removal and better theft tools unlock as you advance, so the fastest cars become keepable later in the game.
- **Register before upgrading.** Spend money on performance parts only after the car is registered to you, or you risk losing the whole build.
