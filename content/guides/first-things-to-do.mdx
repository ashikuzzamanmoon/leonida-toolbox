GTA 6 opens in the Leonida Keys and takes its time opening up. These ten things, drawn from the first post-launch guides, will make your opening hours smoother and set you up for the long game. Spoiler-free.

## 1. Make a post-prologue save

The story introduction is long and cannot be skipped. Once the opening missions are done, make a manual save at Jason's house. If you ever start a second playthrough, you can jump straight past the intro. GTA 6 has no mission replay, so manual saves are your only way back.

## 2. Stash your cash in Jason's bedroom

If you die with money on you, you lose all of it. As soon as you earn your first mission pay, hide it under the loose planks in Jason's bedroom. Money in the stash stays safe no matter what happens to you.

## 3. Hold on to your money

Early cash is scarce, and some story objectives need it, from saving for the future to buying flowers for your partner. Skip the impulse shopping in the first hours and keep a reserve.

## 4. Buy a suppressor at Ammu-Nation

Suppressors make almost every gunfight easier, since they nearly eliminate the noise of your shots. Visit an Ammu-Nation store early and fit one to your main weapon. While you are there, pick up a shotgun: it is the affordable early longarm, and you start the game with only a single gun.

## 5. Stock up on Zombix

Zombix is your healing item, and you can carry up to five. Buy a full stock at a grocery or convenience store before any mission with shooting in it. Running dry mid-mission is a bad way to learn the system.

## 6. Explore Key Lento before rushing to Vice City

The game starts in Key Lento for a reason. It has fully explorable interiors, including the Rusty Anchor and Cheeky Mutineer bars, and some of the best early side content in the game. Do the side activities here while you have free time: underwater treasure diving with Kenton, fishing with Cal, and errands for your neighbor Shirley.

## 7. Feed the snake and shoot some hoops

Two easy ways to raise your focus stat early:

- **Feed Jason's pet snake.** Its terrarium sits near the kitchen in his living room. Feeding it gives 3% focus, once per day.
- **Play basketball.** There is a hoop below Jason's house, near the stairs, with a ball nearby on the ground. Each basket is worth 2% focus, up to five times.

## 8. Learn the crime system

GTA 6 rewards speed and punishes greed. Practice hot-wiring cars, knocking out guards and hitting weak spots in focus mode until they feel natural. When robbing a store, go for the cash register and any safe at the back; shaking down customers usually yields only a few dollars. And when you hear sirens, stop looting and find an exit.

## 9. Buy a garage for your vehicles

Stolen cars you like will not survive long parked on the street. Buy a garage early so you can keep the vehicles you want. See [Best Cars](/guides/best-cars) for what is worth keeping once you have somewhere to put it.

## 10. Push the main story before wandering

It is tempting to explore, but the game gates most of its systems behind story progress. You cannot sell stolen cars, use RydeMe fast travel or access most side activities until you are partway through Chapter 1. Play the story until the world opens up, then go and get lost in it.

For the full basics, read the [Beginner's Guide](/guides/beginners-guide). When you are ready to chase the story, the [Story Mission List](/guides/story-missions) shows how the campaign is laid out.
