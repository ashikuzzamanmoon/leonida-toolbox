GTA 6 has two playable protagonists, Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos. Unlike GTA 5, where you could swap between three characters almost from the start, GTA 6 unlocks free switching partway through the story. This guide explains when it unlocks, how to do it, and the rules around it.

## How to switch characters

Once switching is unlocked, use the Partner Menu:

- **PS5:** hold Right on the D-pad, then press R1.
- **Xbox Series X|S:** hold Right on the D-pad, then press RB.

The character profiles appear in the bottom-right corner. The highlighted profile is your active character. Hold the D-pad button rather than tapping it: a single press of Right detonates placed explosives, so only a held input opens the Partner Menu.

The menu works on foot, in cars, on boats and in aircraft. It also shows Jason and Lucia's current relationship status.

## When switching unlocks

Free switching unlocks in Chapter 2, after you complete the **Breakfast Run** mission, roughly 10 or more hours into the game. Until then, the story picks the protagonist for you. The unlock path runs:

1. **Prologue.** You play as Lucia in prison, then as Jason in the Keys. After Gone Fishing, the open world opens with Jason as your only playable character.
2. **Day Release.** Lucia joins Jason for the first time, but control returns to Jason afterwards because Lucia is out on parole.
3. **End of Chapter 1.** Lucia becomes playable in the open world for the first time after Chapter 1 concludes, starting with the What's In The Bag? mission.
4. **Breakfast Run.** This Chapter 2 mission teaches the Partner Menu swap. From here on, you can freely switch outside character-locked missions.

## Instant swaps and summoning your partner

When Jason and Lucia are together in the same scene, the swap is instant. In a car, switching moves you straight from the driver's seat to the passenger seat and back. When they are apart, the switch plays a short aerial camera transition across the map before handing over control.

You can also use the Partner Menu to **summon your partner** to your location. They usually arrive within a few seconds, sometimes bringing a car. To bring the pair back together, hold Right on the D-pad and press Triangle on PS5 or Y on Xbox. The menu also has a split up option to send them their separate ways.

## Things to know about switching

- **Some missions are locked to one character.** Even after free switching unlocks, certain missions require Jason or Lucia specifically. If a character is greyed out in the Partner Menu, there is an in-game reason, such as them being on another mission.
- **The inactive character keeps living.** While you play as Jason, Lucia goes about her day, and vice versa. If you just swapped away from a character, they may call a RydeMe and leave the area, which can briefly grey out the swap menu.
- **Switching is a shortcut.** If you finish a job with Lucia far from home, check where Jason is. Swapping to him can save a long drive.
- **The story sometimes swaps for you.** Occasionally the game automatically shifts protagonist as a mission's narrative demands it.
- **Some side missions need a specific character.** For example, Kirk's final Skunk Ape mission is Lucia only, while Bret's hunting chain is Jason only. See [Strangers and Side Missions](/guides/side-missions).

## Switching not working?

Almost every case comes down to one of two things: you have not reached Breakfast Run in Chapter 2 yet, or the current mission has locked you to one character. Push the main story forward and check again. See the [Story Mission List](/guides/story-missions) for where you are in the campaign.
