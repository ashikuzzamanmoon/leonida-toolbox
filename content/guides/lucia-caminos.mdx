Lucia Caminos is one of GTA 6's two playable protagonists, alongside Jason Duval, and the first female lead in the Grand Theft Auto series. This guide covers her backstory, her skills and how she plays.

## Backstory

Lucia grew up in Liberty City. Her father taught her to fight as soon as she could walk, and life has been throwing punches at her ever since. Fighting for her family landed her in the Leonida Penitentiary, and sheer luck got her out. Her lesson from prison: only smart moves from now on.

She wants the good life her mother dreamed of since their days in Liberty City, and she is prepared to take matters into her own hands to get it. A life with Jason could be her way out. Rockstar's official quote for her sums up her worldview: "The only thing that matters is who you know and what you got."

## Personality

Lucia is the calculating half of the duo. Where Jason is impulsive and instinctive, she is strategic: she cases a target, spots the vulnerabilities and builds a plan. She is rowdy and kind, but takes no nonsense from anyone. She is also a trained fighter who can hold her own in a street fight, dodge punches and overpower opponents much larger than herself.

## Skills and abilities

- **Fighting.** Trained by her father from early childhood. She is dangerous in close quarters and favors speed and precision.
- **Planning.** Her real talent is strategy. She reads people and situations fast, which is what makes the duo's bigger jobs work.
- **Sidearm.** Her pistol of choice is a Klose K17. The Ultimate Edition adds a personalized, engraved version.
- **Fitness.** She trains regularly: gameplay footage shows her bench-pressing and working the heavy bag at the gym. Away from crime, she works out and goes kayaking.

## Lucia in the story

**Light spoilers for the opening follow.** The game opens with Lucia in the Leonida Penitentiary, meeting her correctional social worker Stefanie, and the prologue shows the failed robbery that put her there. After her release, she reunites with Jason and the two try to build a new life together, until an easy score goes wrong and pulls them into a conspiracy across Leonida. For the full plot, see [Story Explained](/guides/story-explained).

## Playing as Lucia

You first control Lucia in the prologue, then again in story missions such as Day Release and Tourist Trap. She becomes playable in the open world after Chapter 1 ends, and free switching between her and Jason unlocks in Chapter 2 after the Breakfast Run mission. See [How Character Switching Works](/guides/character-switching).

A few things to know when playing as Lucia:

- **Some missions are Lucia only.** Kirk's final Skunk Ape mission, Help Kirk Find Mr. Mangrove, requires her, as does Andy's Cellerica questline.
- **Ultimate Edition extras.** Sara's Unisex Salon offers makeup and nails for Lucia, the Electric Fang tattoo parlor has exclusive designs, and Stock 305 carries exclusive looks.
- **Focus mode.** Like Jason, Lucia can slow time in shootouts to target weak spots, and focus highlights loot while exploring.

## Relationships

- **Jason Duval:** her partner in crime and in life. Their relationship is a central system in the game: how you treat it shapes both the story and the ending. See the [Endings Guide](/guides/endings).
- **Her mother:** back in Liberty City, whose dream of a good life drives Lucia's ambition.
- **Stefanie:** her correctional social worker at the Leonida Penitentiary.
