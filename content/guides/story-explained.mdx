GTA 6 is a love story wrapped in a crime epic. This guide explains the premise and the setup, using Rockstar's official synopsis and early post-launch reporting. The first sections are spoiler-free; the chapter summary below the warning covers the early plot.

## The premise

Rockstar's official synopsis, published with Trailer 2 in May 2025, says it in three sentences:

Jason and Lucia have always known the deck is stacked against them. But when an easy score goes wrong, they find themselves on the darkest side of the sunniest place in America, in the middle of a criminal conspiracy stretching across the state of Leonida, forced to rely on each other more than ever if they want to make it out alive.

The game is set in Leonida, Rockstar's fictional Florida, with Vice City at its heart. Rockstar has openly drawn on the Bonnie and Clyde story: two people in love, escalating from small-time crime into something far larger and more dangerous than they expected.

## The two leads

**Jason Duval** grew up around grifters and crooks. After a stint in the Army trying to leave his troubled teens behind, he ended up in the Leonida Keys working for local drug runners. He wants an easy life, but things keep getting harder. See the full [Jason Duval guide](/guides/jason-duval).

**Lucia Caminos** grew up in Liberty City, where her father taught her to fight as soon as she could walk. Fighting for her family landed her in the Leonida Penitentiary, and sheer luck got her out. She wants the good life her mother dreamed of, and she is prepared to take matters into her own hands to get it. She is the franchise's first female protagonist. See the full [Lucia Caminos guide](/guides/lucia-caminos).

## The themes

A core theme, confirmed by Rockstar North co-studio head Rob Nelson in an Edge cover feature shortly before launch, is **quitting crime**. Jason and Lucia are trying to give their relationship another shot and build a fresh start. As Nelson put it, that relationship is "yours to keep or yours to lose." How you play shapes both their relationship and the ending, through a closeness scale, a criminal profile that tracks how cleanly or recklessly you operate, and a shared family savings account.

## Spoiler warning

**Everything below covers the setup and early plot, into Chapter 2.** If you want to go in cold, stop here.

## How the story opens

The game opens with Lucia being arrested after a failed store robbery, the same sequence shown in the trailers. When she phones Jason from prison, the story shifts to him. The two had split up over disagreements, but Lucia hopes to reunite with him after her release and start a new life together.

Before Lucia's release, Jason works for Brian Heder, an ageing local drug dealer, trafficking goods and handling problems, while renovating a house Brian provided to prepare for their new start.

## The job that goes wrong

Their quiet life does not last. Lucia's former cellmate Kate and her wealthy boyfriend Mattia offer the couple a well-paying job: retrieve a package from a tourist centre locker. The police arrest them during the operation, and dirty cops then attack them during interrogation, leaving Jason seriously injured. Lucia is attacked by masked assailants when she goes home to get emergency funds, and both groups are after the same package.

Hunted from all sides, the couple flee to Port Gellhorn in Kelly County to lay low. Inside the package they find an ancient statue and an encrypted hard drive, and what it holds, and why so many parties want it, becomes a key mystery driving the story.

## Where it goes from there

To make ends meet, the pair carry out a series of robberies across the county. When the money dries up, they head to Vice City to seek help from Dre'Quan, one of Jason's old high school friends. There they meet more local figures and criminal groups, including Dre'Quan's boss Boobie Ike, and join a crew led by Raul, an experienced bank robber, which draws them into larger gang conflicts and the wider conspiracy.

The campaign runs across a prologue and five chapters, with 91 to 93 story missions.
