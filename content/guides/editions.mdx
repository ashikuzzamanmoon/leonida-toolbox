GTA 6 comes in two editions: Standard at $79.99 and Ultimate at $99.99. There is no collector's edition that includes the game. This guide covers what each edition contains and whether the $20 upgrade is worth it.

## Standard Edition: $79.99

The Standard Edition is the base game and nothing else. It includes the full single-player story, starring Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos, on PS5 or Xbox Series X|S. There is no online mode at launch in either edition.

Standard Edition owners can buy the Ultimate Edition Upgrade later, through the PlayStation or Xbox store, so choosing Standard now does not lock you out of the extra content permanently.

## Ultimate Edition: $99.99

The Ultimate Edition includes the base game plus the Ultimate Edition Upgrade: a set of vehicles, weapons, shops and cosmetics that Rockstar says are threaded across the story, with new items uncovered behind each chapter. The full list:

**Vehicles**

- '95 Grotti Cheetah sports car
- '67 Vapid Dominator Buggy, plus the Paradise Garage in Watson Bay
- Shitzu Squalo boat, docked at Washington Beach
- Jason's Safehouse Vehicles: an army fatigue Dinka Enduro motorcycle and a Crest Kayak
- Ganado Retro Build: exclusive mods for Jason's Vapid Ganado pickup

**Weapons**

- Hawk and Little Morgan Revolvers, a matched his-and-hers pair with Vice City styling
- Personalized weapon variants: engraved sidearms for Jason's Girardi ES9 pistol and Lucia's Klose K17 pistol

**Shops and locations**

- Rideout Customs mod shop
- One-Eyed Willie's mod shop
- Sara's Unisex Salon, with styles for both Jason and Lucia
- Stock 305 clothing store
- Electric Fang tattoo parlor

**Cosmetics and activities**

- Vice City Styles: exclusive outfits, tattoos and accessories
- Goodtime Gear apparel
- PTT Youngin$ Compound
- Classic Car Collection: a questline from local fixer Wyman to track down and restore abandoned classic cars

Note that five in-game shops are exclusive to the Ultimate Edition. Standard players cannot use them unless they buy the upgrade.

## Pre-order bonus: Vintage Vice City Pack

Players who bought the game before November 20, 2026 received the Vintage Vice City Pack, regardless of edition. It includes a '55 Vapid Stanier sedan, a garage on Ocean Beach, outfits, hairstyles and a weapon pattern. That window has now closed for new buyers.

Digital pre-orders also included one free month of GTA+, which adds $500,000 to your bank balance in GTA Online and access to the GTA+ Games Library of classic Rockstar titles.

## Physical copies have no disc

There are no traditional physical copies of GTA 6. The boxed version contains a download code and a double-sided poster, not a disc. The poster shows the game's logo on one side and a pin-up character on the other.

Rockstar also sells a separate line of Vice City merchandise, including a Collector's Box for $399.99. It does not include the game.

## Which edition should you buy?

- **Standard** is the right choice if you just want the story. The Ultimate extras are mostly vehicles, cosmetics and shops, and you can upgrade later.
- **Ultimate** is worth the $20 if you want the exclusive vehicles, the extra shops and the Classic Car Collection questline, or if you like collecting cars and customizing them. The Dominator Buggy and its garage are also handy for off-road areas like Mount Kalaga.

Either way, the story is the same. For help once you start, see the [Beginner's Guide](/guides/beginners-guide).
