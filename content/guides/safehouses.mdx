GTA 6 changes how homes work. You cannot buy a safehouse. Instead, Jason and Lucia's homes change as the story moves on, much like the camps in Red Dead Redemption 2. Rockstar's Rob Nelson explained the reason: the pair are on the run, so settling down and buying houses would not fit their story. What you can buy is garages, which are how you build a vehicle collection.

## How safehouses work

Safehouses are where you save, change clothes and rest. They are also far more interactive than in past GTA games: you can open the fridge, watch TV, turn lights on and off, sit on the couch, and get a haircut (shorter only). Jason and Lucia share one home, and each keeps their own schedule inside. Walk in and you may find your partner asleep, cooking, or already out.

The safehouses confirmed so far, in story order:

- **Jason's house, Key Lento.** The first home. It belongs to Brian Heder, a veteran smuggler, who lets Jason live there rent-free in exchange for help with local shakedowns. Lucia moves in after she gets out of prison. See [Leonida Keys & Islands](/guides/leonida-keys).
- **The Bayside apartment, Vice City.** Their home after the move to Vice City in Chapter 3, on Catalan Boulevard near Bayside.
- **Ocean Beach.** In Chapter 4 they move on to an Ocean Beach home.

The Starlet Motel room in Port Gellhorn, seen in the first trailer, is a story location. Whether it works as a safehouse is unconfirmed.

Whether old safehouses stay accessible after you move on is not yet confirmed. To be safe, pick up any collectibles in Jason's Key Lento house before the end of Chapter 1.

## Garages: the property you buy

Garages are the only property you can purchase. After the Chapter 2 mission Daily Driver, the **Vice City Real Estate** app appears on your phone, and all eight garages are bought through it.

- There are **8 garages for sale**, costing **$160,000 in total**. The cheapest is Mitchell Auto Center in Kelly County at $4,000; the most expensive is Atlantic Court Garage in Ocean Beach at $40,000.
- Owning all 8 unlocks the **Little Boxes** trophy.
- Each garage space holds another registered vehicle. With every space taken, you must sell a car before another can become yours.
- Four more garages come free through the story, bringing the base game total to 12.

To make a stolen car yours, register it first: with Moses in Chapter 2, then at any Pay 'n' Spray. You can also leave a stolen car in the parking spot outside your current safehouse to keep it temporarily.

## Bonus garages from editions

Two extra garages come with what you bought, not with in-game money. Both include a weapon locker and a secure place to drop stolen goods for fencing:

- **Shore Court Garage, Ocean Beach.** Included with the Vintage Vice City Pack, which came with every edition bought before November 20, 2026. It holds one vehicle and comes with a '55 Vapid Stanier.
- **Paradise Garage, Watson Bay.** Included with the Ultimate Edition, sized for the edition's '67 Vapid Dominator Buggy.

The Ultimate Edition also parks a Dinka Enduro motorcycle and a Crest Kayak at Jason's safehouse.

## What about businesses?

No buyable businesses that earn passive income, like the ones in GTA: Vice City or GTA V, have turned up in GTA 6 so far. Property spending goes on garages, vehicles and gear. See the [Money Guide](/guides/money-guide) for how to fund it all.
