Motorcycles are the fastest way through Vice City traffic, and the quickest bike in the game is estimated to be faster than any car. Around 15 to 16 motorcycles have been confirmed so far, spanning superbikes, dirt bikes, choppers and scooters. As with cars, Rockstar has published no official stats, so speeds below are estimates and the fastest-bike crown is still disputed.

## Fastest bike: still disputed

Two sources currently disagree on the fastest motorcycle in GTA 6:

- **Pro Game Guides** puts the **Principe Alvino V1** first at an estimated 155 mph (249 km/h), a new Ducati Panigale V2-style superbike.
- **Rankings based on real-world counterparts** put the **Nagasaki Carbon RS** (Ducati 1199 Panigale basis) first, with the Dinka Double-T close behind.

Early in-game stat ratings lean toward the Alvino V1, which is listed with a higher speed rating than the Carbon RS. Until both bikes are timed on the same route, treat the Alvino V1 as the provisional fastest and the Carbon RS as its closest rival.

## Best bikes by type

| Type | Best picks |
| --- | --- |
| Superbike | Principe Alvino V1, Nagasaki Carbon RS, Dinka Double-T, Pegassi Bati 801 |
| Dirt bike | Maibatsu Manchez, Maibatsu Sanchez, Dinka Enduro |
| Chopper / cruiser | Western Nightblade, Western Zombie Bobber, LCC Avarus |
| Scooter | Pegassi Faggio, Nagasaki Nimbe |

**Superbikes** are the fastest machines in the game and the best choice for getaways, cutting through traffic that would trap a car. The Alvino V1, Carbon RS and Double-T all appear in the trailers, and Jason rides a green Alvino V1 in official screenshots.

**Dirt bikes** rule the rough ground of Ambrosia, Grassrivers and Mount Kalaga. The Manchez and Sanchez are light, tough and built for mud and sand. The Dinka Enduro is a 1970s-style dual-sport that comes with the Ultimate Edition and handles well both off-road and on asphalt.

**Choppers and cruisers** are slower but heavy and showy, popular with the biker gangs around Leonida. The Nightblade and Zombie Bobber are the picks here.

**Scooters** are for city commuting and tight streets. The Faggio is back, alongside the electric Nagasaki Nimbe and the three-wheeled Maibatsu Boomazoom mobility scooter.

## Why bikes matter in GTA 6

- **Getaways.** Robbing a bank vault instantly earns 4 stars, and guides consistently recommend a motorcycle to cut through traffic during the escape.
- **Races.** Dirt bike races are a dedicated activity type, with courses across Leonida.
- **Cheap transport.** Bikes are easy to steal and cheap to keep, and they fit the narrow streets and alleyways of Vice City better than any car.

One caution: bikes offer no protection in a crash or a shootout, and they cannot carry loot bags, so they are useless for large robbery hauls. For the fastest cars instead, see [Fastest Car in GTA 6](/guides/fastest-car), and for the full vehicle picture, [Best Cars in GTA 6](/guides/best-cars).
