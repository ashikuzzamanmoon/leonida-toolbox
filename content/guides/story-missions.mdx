**Spoiler warning:** mission names can hint at story events. This guide lists chapter names and the opening missions of each chapter. Read only up to your current chapter if you want to stay unspoiled.

GTA 6's campaign is told across a prologue and five chapters. Most outlets that have mapped the story through the in-game Story recap tab count **93 story missions**. Some count 91, because two jobs in Chapter 2 are optional. The count is now confirmed: the in-game Story recap tab lists 93 missions, including the two optional jobs in Chapter 2.

## Chapters at a glance

| Chapter | Title | Missions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Just Like Paradise | 22 |
| 2 | Paradise Lost | 13 (2 optional) |
| 3 | Tits Ass Drug Money | 26 |
| 4 | Vice City, Baby | 25 |
| 5 | Top of the World, Ma | 7 |
| **Total** | | **93** |

Chapters 3 and 4 are the meat of the game, with 51 missions between them. Chapter 5 is a short finale of seven missions. Rockstar has said the structure is deliberately cinematic: chapters 1 and 2 are shorter, 3 and 4 are the longest, and chapter 5 tightens up to close the game out.

## Chapter 1: Just Like Paradise (22 missions)

The story opens with Lucia's prologue, then follows Jason in the Leonida Keys as he prepares for Lucia's release from prison. The opening missions are:

1. Prologue
2. Gone Fishing
3. Gear Up
4. Just A Scratch
5. Lab Fees

Gone Fishing unlocks Jason's house in Key Lento, your base for the chapter. Chapter 1 is where the game teaches its systems, and where you first meet Jason's circle: his best friend Cal Hampton, and Brian Heder, the local dealer he works for.

## Chapter 2: Paradise Lost (13 missions)

Chapter 2 takes Jason and Lucia to Kelly County, starting in Port Gellhorn. It opens with What's In The Bag? Free character switching unlocks during this chapter, after the Breakfast Run mission. Two of the chapter's 13 missions are optional jobs, which is why some lists count 91 missions overall instead of 93.

## Chapter 3: Tits Ass Drug Money (26 missions)

The longest chapter, opening with Welcome To Vice City. Jason and Lucia arrive in Vice City proper, meet new contacts and crew members, and the story's conspiracy starts to take shape. The chapter builds to a large heist.

## Chapter 4: Vice City, Baby (25 missions)

The second-longest chapter, opening with Ocean Beach. The conspiracy deepens and the jobs get bigger, building to another major heist.

## Chapter 5: Top of the World, Ma (7 missions)

The finale, closing with The End Of The Road. Short and focused after the sprawl of chapters 3 and 4.

## How to track your progress

Open the map and press Right to see a list of available story missions, with mandatory objectives at the top and optional ones below. You can set a waypoint to any mission from there. The in-game Story recap tab also lists every mission in order, which is how the counts above were verified.

## Things to know

- **No mission replay.** Once a story mission is complete, you cannot run it again on the same save. Keep manual saves before big moments if you want to revisit them.
- **Some missions are locked to one character.** Even after free switching unlocks, certain missions require Jason or Lucia specifically. See [How Character Switching Works](/guides/character-switching).
- **Side missions are separate.** Strangers and Freaks questlines, treasure dives and other optional content sit outside this count. See [Strangers and Side Missions](/guides/side-missions).
- **The ending depends on how you play.** Your choices, your relationship and your criminal profile all shape the finale. See the [Endings Guide](/guides/endings) for what is known, spoiler-light.
