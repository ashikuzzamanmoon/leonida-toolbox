GTA 6 has a straightforward save system, but two things catch players out: there is no mission replay menu, and your save does not move between PlayStation and Xbox. This guide covers how saving works, how to back up your progress, and what cross-progression means for GTA 6.

## How to save

- **Manual save:** open the pause menu (Options on PS5, Menu on Xbox), go to **Game**, then **Save Game**, and pick a slot. You can save almost anywhere in free roam: on a sidewalk, on a boat, or at your safehouse. There is no need to drive home first.
- **15 manual slots.** IGN confirmed the game gives you 15 manual save slots.
- **Autosave:** the game autosaves after you finish a mission. It lives as a separate entry in the Load Game list, labeled (Autosave) with the mission name and your completion percentage. You cannot turn autosave off, and it will not save you mid-mission.
- **Load:** pause menu, Game, then Load Game. The list shows your manual saves and the autosave with mission names, percentages and timestamps.

## Save often, in fresh slots

Two habits will save you grief:

- **There is no mission replay menu.** To replay a finished mission, you must reload an earlier manual save, because the autosave overwrites itself. Save in a new slot at the start of each chapter, and in a fresh slot before any big choice you might want to try both ways.
- **Autosave only fires when a mission ends.** Anything you did after the last mission is lost if the game crashes or the power cuts out. Crashes at launch have cost some players hours of progress. Save manually before you stop playing.
- **Cheats block saving.** While a cheat is active, you cannot save at all. See [Cheats](/guides/cheats).

## Cloud saves

Cloud backup works inside each console family, not between them:

- **PS5:** saves live on the console. Cloud storage is a PlayStation Plus benefit (up to 100 GB of PS5 saved data) and syncs when you close the game or put the console in rest mode. Turn it on under Settings > Saved Data and Game/App Settings > Saved Data (PS5) > Sync Saved Data. PS5 saves cannot be copied to a USB drive; USB copying is for PS4 game saves only.
- **Xbox Series X|S:** saved games sync to your Microsoft account automatically while you are connected to the Xbox network, with no paid membership needed.

## Cross-progression and cross-save

**Rockstar has not announced any way to carry a GTA 6 save between PS5 and Xbox.** Treat your platform choice as permanent for this playthrough:

- **No cross-save.** Buying the game on PS5 and later on Xbox means starting over.
- **No cross-buy.** The PS5 and Xbox versions are separate purchases on separate stores. Physical copies contain a download code for one console's store only.
- **Trophies and achievements stay with the platform.** PS5 trophies and Xbox achievements are separate lists tied to each platform's account.
- **No crossplay either.** GTA 6 is a single-player game at launch, so there is no multiplayer mode for PS5 and Xbox players to share. PlayStation's own FAQ puts it in one line: "Grand Theft Auto VI is a single-player experience."

## What does work across devices

- **Remote Play** is fully supported on PlayStation and Xbox, so you can play your console's game on another screen.
- **Xbox Cloud Gaming** lets Xbox One owners stream the game at Series S settings, and works on Series X|S too. Your cloud save follows you between Xbox consoles.
