Players found GTA 6's first easter eggs within hours of the game unlocking in New Zealand on launch day. Rockstar never confirms easter eggs, so this guide lists each find with the outlet that reported it, and marks anything that rests on a single unverified clip as unconfirmed. Expect this list to grow fast.

For hidden places and stashes rather than references, see [Hidden Locations](/guides/hidden-locations).

## The Skunk Ape (Bigfoot)

Leonida's Bigfoot is the Skunk Ape, Florida's own cryptid: a tall, hairy figure wrapped in a cloud of foul fumes. GameSpot and PC Gamer reported the encounter on launch day.

- It roams the **Grassrivers swamps**, most often reported on the road on the east side of Cottonmouth Lake toward Gambit Bay, north of Key Lento.
- Follow the roar and use Focus mode to track its noises. It bolts as soon as you get close.
- Following its trail leads to a carved wooden effigy tied to a tree. Interacting with it releases noxious fumes that knock you out, followed by a short cutscene.
- A handwritten poster in Key Lento, headed "Have You Seen Skunk Ape???", leads to a side quest strand with a character named Kirk. His first mission is called Skunk Apeology 101, hunting a creature called Mr. Mangrove. The strand counts toward the Strangers & Freaks trophy.

## A literal Easter egg

GTA 6 has a literal easter egg. Climb the tallest cell tower in Vice City, a climb players say takes six to seven minutes, and you will find a nest with painted Easter eggs at the top. Jason or Lucia can pick one up and throw it, which plays a short cutscene below. GameSpot reported the find but has not named the exact tower yet.

## Dexter's apartment

On Washington Island in Vice City, players found an apartment complex modeled on Dexter Morgan's Miami home from the TV show Dexter. IGN and Dexerto reported it on launch day.

- Rockstar named the building **Trinity Apartments**, a nod to the Trinity Killer, the villain of the show's fourth season.
- You can walk around the complex but cannot enter the apartment itself. The air conditioner unit sits where the show puts it.
- On the roof sits a kill kit: a knife, rope, tape and a syringe.
- Nearby, a boat resembling Dexter's "Slice of Life" is docked and can be stolen.

## The Florida Joker

The Florida Joker (Lawrence Sullivan) is credited in GTA 6 — players spotted his name in the game's credits after launch. Rockstar has not said what role, if any, he plays in the game, so treat any claimed in-game sighting as unconfirmed until a verified clip or screenshot pins it down.

## Nods to past Rockstar games

- **Red Dead Redemption.** GameSpot found a Western novel about a cowboy in New Austin at Jacob's Tavern in Millport. A "Rad Dads Redemption Tour" poster has also been spotted.
- **GTA IV.** Early in Chapter 1, the book by Jason's back door is a memoir by a former member of Liberty City organized crime, mentioning events from 2008, the year GTA IV is set.
- **GTA V.** Wade from GTA V appears on the Final Act podcast, and Fabien LaRouche shows up in a gym magazine.
- **Mount Chiliad.** Two drinkers in an early story bar talk about the bunker with jetpacks under Mount Chiliad, a nod to GTA V's most famous mystery.
- **Tommy Vercetti.** The Vintage Vice City Pack, the pre-order bonus, includes a weapon pattern inspired by Tommy Vercetti's palm-tree shirt from GTA: Vice City.

## Unconfirmed: UFO abduction and the giant crab

Two more finds are circulating but rest on single clips so far, so treat them as unconfirmed:

- **UFO abduction.** A video shows a UFO event in Kelly County, just east of Port Gellhorn, after 2am in-game: a parked car with flickering lights, a trail into the woods, and an abduction scene that ends with Jason waking in a field. It would fit the hidden trophy "The Truth Is Out There," whose icon shows a flying saucer beaming up a cow. Other reports place UFO activity in Chapter 2 while playing as Lucia. The exact trigger is not yet confirmed.
- **Giant crab.** A launch-day clip shows a rare cutscene with a giant monster crab. Nobody has explained how the player triggered it yet.
