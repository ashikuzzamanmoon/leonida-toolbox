GTA 6 runs at **30 frames per second on every console**, with no performance mode, no quality mode, and no graphics toggle in the menu. Rockstar confirmed the 30fps target before launch, and Digital Foundry's tests on November 18, 2026 confirmed it on all four consoles: PS5, PS5 Pro, Xbox Series X and Xbox Series S. The reason, Rockstar says, is the open-world simulation: the CPU cost of running Leonida's systems is what caps the frame rate, not the graphics hardware.

This guide compares the consoles and covers the display settings you can actually change.

## Console comparison

| Console | Frame rate | Internal resolution | Upscaling | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| PS5 Pro | 30fps | Around 1440p | PSSR to 4K | Holds 30fps almost everywhere; ray-traced shadows; sharpest image |
| PS5 | 30fps | Around 1440p | TAA to 4K | Small dips while driving and in gunfights; big explosions can pull it into the low to mid 20s |
| Xbox Series X | 30fps | Around 1440p | TAA to 4K | Steady 30 most of the time, upper 20s in fast chases; some camera ghosting reported |
| Xbox Series S | 30fps | Around 720p | FSR 4 to 1080p | Often mid to upper 20s while driving or fighting; worst scenes around 20fps; reduced crowd density and draw distance |

The PS5 Pro is the best console version: Digital Foundry described its frame rate as "unwavering," and it is the only console with ray-traced shadows. But even the Pro cannot reach 60fps. Rockstar's technical production director Marc Chambers explained that the Pro's CPU is only slightly faster than the base PS5, and that is not enough to run the game's simulations at a higher frame rate.

## Settings you can change

The Display tab has no graphics or resolution option, but these settings do exist and are worth adjusting:

- **Motion Blur Strength.** Setting it to 0 makes the camera ghosting some players report less noticeable, and makes the 30fps motion feel cleaner.
- **HDR.** On or off depending on your TV. If shadows disappear into solid black, start with the in-game brightness and HDR controls.
- **VRR.** If your display supports variable refresh rate, turning it on makes dropped frames less noticeable. It helps less on Series S, where the frame rate drops further.
- **120Hz output.** On a 120Hz display, the game still targets 30fps, but frame pacing can feel smoother with VRR enabled.
- **Look and Aim Sensitivity.** At 30fps the camera can feel slow to respond. Raising sensitivity helps a little.
- **Effects intensity.** Player Status Effects, Hit Effects and Awareness Effects each have an intensity slider. Lower values reduce screen clutter in combat.

The dark vignette around the edges of the screen cannot be turned off.

## Xbox Cloud Gaming and last-gen consoles

There is no PS4 version and no native Xbox One version. Xbox One owners can only play through **Xbox Cloud Gaming**, which uses Series S graphics settings. Cloud play also works on Xbox Series X|S consoles. GTA 6 is not part of Game Pass, so you still need to buy it.

## What about PC?

There is no PC version dated yet, and no PC graphics settings to discuss. Rockstar has not announced a PC release. See [PC Release](/guides/pc-release) for what is known.

## Known issues at launch

- Some PS5 and PS5 Pro consoles have hit shutdowns in the prologue. Rockstar has not yet commented on a fix.
- Crashes on both platforms have cost some players hours of progress, because autosave only fires when a mission ends. Save manually before you stop playing. See [Saves & Cross-Progression](/guides/saves-cross-progression).
