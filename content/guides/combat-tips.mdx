Combat in GTA 6 is built around cover, positioning and the Jason and Lucia partnership, with a new slow-motion aiming system and a wanted system that remembers what the police know about you. Here is how it all works and how to use it.

## Gunplay basics

- **Use cover.** Combat leans heavily on cover: duck behind furniture and vehicles, vault over car hoods mid-fight, and swap shoulders to shoot around the other side of cover without exposing yourself.
- **Focus mode.** A slow-motion aiming mode, similar to Red Dead Redemption 2's Dead Eye, lets you target specific body parts. The targeting overlay marks lethal shots in red and non-lethal shots around joints in yellow, so disarming an enemy or stopping them without killing them is a real option.
- **No hard lock-on.** GTA 5's auto-aim that snapped onto enemies is gone. Aiming uses aim assist instead, so practice your aim at the shooting range.
- **Quick swap.** You can switch between two carried firearms without opening the weapon wheel.
- **One-handed reloading.** You can reload with one hand while carrying loot bags during robberies.

## Loadout and gear

You can carry two sidearms and two longarms, plus throwables. Extra guns live in your car trunk or an Ammu-Nation locker. The redesigned weapon wheel has separate Weapons and Items tabs: the Items tab covers body armor, food and drink, trauma kits and gear such as binoculars and duffel bags.

- **Buy body armor.** It is sold at Ammu-Nation and matters more than a marginal weapon upgrade.
- **Carry healing.** Trauma kits and food keep you going through long fights.
- **Practice at the range.** Ammu-Nation shooting ranges run timed courses against 12 targets, with time bonuses for hits. Both Jason and Lucia can use them.

## Fighting as a pair

Jason and Lucia are built to operate together. When they are in the same location, switching between them is almost instant, and the character you are not controlling fights alongside you with AI support.

- **Split the fight.** Missions are designed around the pair covering each other, such as one clearing upstairs while the other holds downstairs.
- **Drive and shoot.** During chases, one drives while the other shoots from the window. Switch between them depending on whether driving or shooting needs you more.
- **Split up to escape.** If witnesses saw you as a couple, separating makes you harder to identify.

## Melee

Melee is a real system, not a fallback. Lucia's hand-to-hand combat includes a counter mechanic, and the baseball bat is a strong early melee weapon with long reach. Backyard wrestling is also a side activity if you want to practice up close.

## The wanted system and heat

GTA 6 brings back a six-star wanted level, but the stars are only half the story. Police only respond once a crime is witnessed or an alarm goes off, and a heat system tracks what they know about you.

**Star states:**

- **Hollow stars** — a crime was reported but the suspect is unidentified. Leave immediately.
- **Solid white stars** — police can see you and are pursuing.
- **Flashing stars** — police lost sight of you and are searching your last known area.
- **Red / heat** — the pursuit ended, but police retain identifying details in the area. Return and you can be recognized.

**Heat icons** under the stars show what police know: your appearance, your outfit, your vehicle, your weapon, whether you were a pair, and whether CCTV caught you. Clear them to make yourself unrecognizable:

- **Change clothes** — buy a new outfit or swap from the trunk of your personal vehicle.
- **Change vehicles** — abandon the getaway car, switch to another, or respray at a Pay 'n' Spray.
- **Split up** — if police know you were a pair, separate Jason and Lucia.
- **Drop the weapon** — put it away or store it.
- **Leave the search area** — the hot zone is marked on the map. Stay out of it until the heat fades.

Police tactics escalate with stars: helicopters with searchlights, spike strips, SUVs, SWAT units and K9 units whose dogs can pin you down. Slowing the pursuit helps: shooting out cop car tires slows them, and helicopters may retreat if shot at. Fighting back raises the violence level, so if your goal is escape, break line of sight and disappear rather than standing your ground.
